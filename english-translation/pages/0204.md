# Blossom 67 — The Truth Unknown to the Devotee

It was the wont of that Saiva adherent to invite a guest to a feast on all the important Saivite festivals.

That Saiva devotee didn’t need the assistance of any almanac to realize that it was *Mahāsivarāttiri*.

He wandered around looking for a guest to be fed on that day. His search proved futile. At last, he was heartened to see somebody slumbering in the choultry. He heaved a sigh of relief.

Overcome with joy, he approached him. He addressed that devotee of Lord Siva, who was dozing off with the ritual sacred ash sported on his forehead, chest and folded hands. “Sir, the one of ripeness!” he called a bit loudly.

“Who is that? I am lying here sapped of vitality due to hunger and fatigue. Why are you bothering me?” So saying the devotee got up.

The host replied, “I invite you not to appease your hunger. It is to cleanse myself of my sins. I invite you to a feast in my house.”

“Is it so? Are you offering food? Why the invitation? I don’t require one,” So saying the devotee started walking with the Saivite friend.
