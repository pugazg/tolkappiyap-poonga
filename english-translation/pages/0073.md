# Blossom 18 — Mutal Poruḷ

Darwin’s theory has succeeded in scientifically explaining the human being’s evolution from the monkey. Among this teeming sea of humanity, growing in their multitudes, owing to diversification development and witnessed in this world, somewhere around five thousand years ago, the Tamil race was gradually preparing itself for a systematic form of life.

Leading an unorganized life is poles apart from leading a planned course of living. In order to formulate such a structured life, communication tools are mandatory. Among these communication tools, language has stood the test of time and stands as numero uno.

The races emerging from various parts of the world had been using their languages, but the Tamil race has had the distinction of standardizing its language and developing literature and grammar at a very early stage. The historically renowned Tamil race is credited with providing sheen and glitter to their language two thousand years ago, though the development process itself started some three thousand years earlier.

*Tolkāppiyam*’s *Poruḷatikāram* section, the grammar of literature and life, is a perfect telltale example of that feat. Though the modes of living and patterns of behaviour mentioned in *Tolkāppiyam* were very much in practice much before the *Tolkāppiyam*’s era, we can always recollect with pride that the arduous task of piecing them together and presenting them in the form of a poetic garland was effortlessly accomplished by Tolkāppiyar.
