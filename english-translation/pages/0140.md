# Blossom 42 — Speaking through the Eyes

The confidant was hiding beside the bushes for a long time  
Without causing any embarrassment to the lovers rendezvousing;  
He put a stop even to his cough  
That tended to come out of this throat,  
For that was not the hour to do so.

He was all ears to listen to what they were speaking.  
There was no sound to be heard.

“Where have they gone?  
Are they present or not?”  
Confusion reigned over his head.

He stretched up a little,  
Both of them were seen standing like statues.
