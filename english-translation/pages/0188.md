# Blossom 59 — What did the Heart Say? · continued

Having resolved thus  
She asks her heart:  
‘Is my resolution right?’

But her whining heart says ‘no’,  
“You only pretend to be indifferent to your body and life for society’s sake,  
Whereas your actual desire is to meet him at this very instant.”

***

> Hero’s union she does think of  
> At those lonesome moments  
> When she addresses her heart.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Poruḷiyal*, verse 10)
