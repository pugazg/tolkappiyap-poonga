# Blossom 80 — Fivefold Akam Love: The First Stage of Physical Manifestations · continued

Which the sage, an absolute renunciate, also noticed.

Encounter belongs usually to the warfield.  
But here it is the arena of love’s union.

In the lanes or gardens wearing a deserted look,  
The flower of a girl and the tiger of a young man run into.  
They are mature enough to understand the language of the eyes.

As their four eyes exchange their glances  
- Beads of perspiration appear on the forehead of this woman fit for poetry.

Her head drooped weighs down by abundant shyness.  
Whereas the man doesn’t fail to drop his hints.

That queen of beauty grasps his intension - immediately.  
How eager she is to converse with him!

Here is the picture of this encounter as it constitutes the first phase of the love career of the *Akam* hero and heroine:

The states of mind that there aptly manifest  
As the hero and the heroine are drawn by passion  
In their clandestine pre-marital course are:  
Her receptive posture as the hero’s passionate eyes fall on her,  
Her forehead wearing beads of sweating,  
Her coyness concealing her inward longing and delight  
And her keeping off from others,  
Her heart having been lost to him.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 13)
