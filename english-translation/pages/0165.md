# Blossom 49 — Clandestine Love Career and Wedded Course · continued

Queried the hero.

“It was my confidante’s counsel  
That we should worship and adore God  
For our love to come to fruition without hassles.  
But are you angry about it now?”  
So saying the heroine served food on his leaf plate  
And sat by him to eat together with him.

“How is it that  
The food turns manna with your hand’s touch?  
Not just that.  
The flower too acquires a new fragrance!  
Are all these because of the bliss of God?”  
Wondered the hero.

“It is all because my God dwells in my heart.”

As she said thus,  
Both of them forgot the food cooked and served on the plate,  
Vied with each other  
And relished the lips of each other.

Food taken  
They commented:  
“There is one thing that tastes sweet;  
It is not the manna but the lips!”

Since it was the early days of wedded life  
They made no distinction  
As to whether it was morning, noon or evening  
Or day or night,  
And realized their dreams of passion  
Which they yearned for in their clandestine course.

Realizing that  
He who lives a virtuous life is known as a brahmin,  
They decided by the dictates of their wisdom  
And followd the path of domestic virtue.
