# Blossom 52 — A Woman’s Victory · continued

She thought that if she remained silent not protesting,  
She might end up losing her relationship with her son as well.  
Just at this juncture,  
The son decked in ornaments came calling her ‘O mother!’.  
Her anger raging like a volcano subdued cool as snowfall.  
Spotting her husband she exclaimed, “Oh, here you are!  
Hereafter the boy alone would be the ray of hope dissipating my darkness;  
Today I start for my mother’s house along with him.  
Some men do exist, like the spots on the face of the moon,  
But you are a moon all in spots!

Intiraṉ himself should take a lesson or two from you in the art of winning  
women.

So long I exercised restraint with the fond hope of reforming you.  
You cannot bridle your habit of making merry every day.

My son who looks like the full moon is enough to provide me solace.  
You spoiled the family with a mind perverse,  
Your blotted life would teach you a fitting lesson before long.”

Bursting out thus,  
That speaking portrait was about to exit from the house  
Along with the soothing breeze on her lap.

The husband cried out in pain within,  
With his legs trembling  
As if he were stabbed by a thousand spears and swords in the chest.  
“Let the bygones be bygones!  
You might leave me immediately if it were to happen again.  
I don’t fancy a life without you, my singing bird!  
Like the singing birds let us live without any worry.  
Let’s lead an impeccable life which nobody can fault.  
I will ever remain faithful to you hereafter.”  
Coming around, he fell at her feet.  
“It is not enough if the people here stand witness.  
Let your former concubines also see this scene”, she said.
