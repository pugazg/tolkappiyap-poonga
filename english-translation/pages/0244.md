# Blossom 85 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Sixth Stage of Physical Manifestations · continued

And she is deeply worried over not sighting her lover even for a single moment.  
She is disturbed and asks her confidante as to where he has gone?  
The angst scorches her like the latent heat of the sun.  
She stands helpless and in quite despondency.

When one is at the extreme of either melancholy or mirth,  
One becomes helpless and confused.  
This is not peculiar only to her, but natural to one and all.

* * *

> The states of mind that are revealed in  
> The sixth phase of the clandestine course are:  
> The lonesome heroine turning heartsick  
> At her person bedecked with ornaments,  
> Her falling into a mood of despondency,  
> Her utterances of dismay and distress,  
> And her wearing a sense of helplessness.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 18)
