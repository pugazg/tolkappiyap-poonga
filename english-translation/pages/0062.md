# Blossom 14 — My Son?

The cart-driver holding on the Mayilai bullocks and saying “We’ve arrived at Karuṇaiyānanta Press”, Kaliyaperumāḷ leaning comfortably in the cart, alighted with a bag in his hands.

As soon as Kaliyaperumāḷ entered the room of the manager in the frontier of the printing press, he was greeted by the saluting manager with the chants of “Welcome! Welcome!” and was offered a chair.

Kaliyaperumāḷ extricated five or six big papers from his bag and said, “An auspicious event is slated to take place in our house. That is why I have come here.”

“I am very happy. Seems like son’s marriage. You are here for printing invitation. Went through the news!”

“Yes, the marriage was fixed in a hurry. One thousand invitations must be printed. Groom’s name - Bride’s name and other details are there in these papers! You yourself can make necessary corrections.”

The manager scanned through the papers handed to him and remarked, “Okay! Though it is a self-respect marriage, the Ayyar who wrote this seems to have smeared customary turmeric powder on it and noted down the auspicious time for marriage along with astrological details as in the conventional marriage.”

“Is that so? Has he written the auspicious time? The Ayyar has shown his true colours! It is okay. Though the Ayyar has set an auspicious time for the marriage, it would be conducted on line with the principles of Periyar. Therefore, let the title of the invitation be ‘Wedding Celebration Invitation’.”
