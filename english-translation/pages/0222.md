# Blossom 74 — Four Sources of Disgust · continued

## Vexation (varuttam)

Like the birds that desert the dried-up pond, selfish people leave their friends when the latter lose their wealth. Such victims direct their ire not against the former friends but at their own miseries. Is it not because misery becomes their companion that their friends left them?

## Incapacitation/simplicity (meṇmai)

When the rich lead a simple life, it is valued as simplicity. If simplicity is taken to the extreme and the person takes to wearing torn and crumpled clothes, it is miserliness causing a sense of disgust to others.

* * *

> The four abiding sources of disgust are  
> Decrepitude, sickliness, vexation and incapacitation.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 6)
