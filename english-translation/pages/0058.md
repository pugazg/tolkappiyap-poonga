# Blossom 12 — Word-picture · continued

“What does *Tolkāppiyam* prescribe?”

“Let me throw light upon that. Though eye, shoulder and bosom signify plurality, they need not be referred to as two eyes, two shoulders, two bosoms. They, or for that matter other words like them, can be stated in singular which would very well imply their plurality. If the context demands, they could be presented in plural form as well.”

“Is that so? Is my painting a telltale example of this grammatical norm? Kindly recite the relevant verse.”

“Yes! I will recite it verbatim. What I am going to say is related to words, while your painting is its pictorial representation.”

> Limbs in pairs  
> Such as eye, shoulder and breast  
> Need no plural suffix  
> Unless they are followed by plural verbs.
>
> (*Collatikāram : Kiḷaviyākkam*, verse 62)
