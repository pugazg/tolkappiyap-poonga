# Blossom 47 — Wedded Love

The *karpiyal* (wedded love career) section of *Tolkāppiyam*  
Makes it clear that

The term *karaṇam* signifies marriage.

Commentator Iḷampūraṇar looks at *karaṇam*  
As belonging to brahmins, kings and traders,  
The reason being they are of the upper castes.

He further says,  
A time there was  
When *karaṇam* was practised by the Vēḷāḷars down the ladder too.

Nacciṉārkkiṉiyar too believed in  
The existence of caste distinctions.

Many a scholar today  
Shows with evidences  
The three of the upper stratum  
As meaning the three crowned monarchs  
And the lower stratum their subjects.
