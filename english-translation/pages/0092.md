# Blossom 27 — Joy in Adversity

The hero elopes with the heroine, bound by the spell of love. Their journey extends from the sylvan tract through the arid wastes. This is a scene from *Kalittokai*, seen in the backdrop of *Tolkāppiyam*.

In the early morning, being parted from the mother, she accompanies her lover by holding his hand. She comes across deer and rabbit frolicking among *koṉṟai, kāyā* and *kuruntam* trees. Blossoms of *mullai, kullai* and *paṭavam* are spreading fragrance all around. The peasants rush to sow common millets like *cāmai* and *varaku*. She beholds all the scenes while continuing the journey.

Since the dawn replaces early morning darkness, they can no longer remain hidden from others. The twinkling stars scattered profusely on the sky gravitate us to see them. Even among those innumerable stars, don’t we view the moon first? This most beautiful damsel is that moon! She is like a statue in beauty! She moves like an exquisite art! When she goes along with her lover equally matching in charm and valour, nobody could miss an opportunity to admire that perfect pair.

When they travel a long distance in the desert land, they are trailed by the foster mother, anxious to trace them. In that land, where are seen emaciated elephants and wolves moving around and parched ponds and wells, the foster mother travels listening to the music of the *paṟai* (a percussion instrument) and the *yāḻ* (a stringed musical instrument) even while she eagerly enquires about her daughter and her lover.
