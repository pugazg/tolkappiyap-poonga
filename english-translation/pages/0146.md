# Blossom 44 — The Wet Firewood that Burned

“Though she is not an angel,  
I would say even the angels cannot measure up to her.

Even if one were to search vigorously  
And select the best among one crore women,  
Searching into the present and the future,  
There is nobody quite like this damsel beautiful as the moon  
On the earth or in the myriad worlds *purāṇas* speak of ;  
Definitely we cannot find one of her kind.

You would not deny it I am sure!  
For you have seen her.”

Sitting on the horse-drawn chariot stationary  
He asked his confidant dear as life a thousand times.

No boredom writ on the face,  
The friend acquiescent said,
