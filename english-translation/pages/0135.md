# Blossom 40 — The Tamiḻ Concept of Love

> Look at the commentary of the passionate Tamiḻ scholar Veḷḷaivāraṇar  
> On the *Tolkāppiyam* verse on the nature of clandestine love!  
> We will understand  
> Why Tolkāppiyar refers here to the eight kinds of marriage in the Aryan land.
>
> These kinds stand flawed,  
> Unacceptable to the impeccable Tamiḻ mind;  
> They might suit only the Aryan culture  
> In their entirety;  
> And just one or two of them correspond to the Tamiḻ ethos -  
> So says the learned Tamiḻ commentator.
>
> What then are those eight kinds of marriage?  
> *Piramam* is gifting a girl of twelve  
> To a bachelor nearing fifty years of age;  
> Giving a girl in marriage to the brother-in-law by relation  
> With the mutual parental consent
