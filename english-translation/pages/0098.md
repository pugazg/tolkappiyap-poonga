# Blossom 28 — The Call of Duty and the Predicament of the Hero · continued

> Addresses his charioteer urging him to make it swift;  
> As he parts from his love  
> For purpose of security and protection of the state;  
> And,  
> As he importunes her  
> After his return from prostitutes  
> And seeks resolution of her sulking.  
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse 41)
