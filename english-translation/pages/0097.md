# Blossom 28 — The Call of Duty and the Predicament of the Hero · continued

The enemy’s strength, his army’s strength, and ally’s strength, while simultaneously weighing in foot-soldiers,cavalry, and elephant force also does not go unnoticed. In spite of all these reflections flashing across his mind, and his determination to encounter the odds and triumph over them, he cannot leave without seeking farewell from his darling. Since it involves the security of the country, the lady bids farewell wishing him victory. She might have shed tears like the Ganga and the Cauvery.

On the battlefield like a tiger in the midst of elephants, he comes out with all the strength of determination.

He proves his mettle, and the hero demonstrates that he is second to none.

While in the war-camp, his fair lady disturbs him in the dream. Since craving and pining for her in dreams would dampen his heroic vigour, he pushes her aside.

Victory comes with the hoisting of the flag of victory. He hastens the chariot driver. “The sweet lady of mine would be waiting; her passionate kisses would heal my injuries. Go fast”, he says. “Horses, may God bless you to run like a storm and convey me to my wife”, he requests from the core of his heart.

On the way, occasionally, on encountering a prostitute he makes merry. Since this news would reach the ears of his love waiting at home, it would lead to sulking.

He should resolve that sulking also and row the boat of marital life in the river of happiness. The poetic hero has many such responsibilities and duties, and predicaments as well:

> And the rewards attendant therein;

> As he responds to the calling of being a royal emissary;
