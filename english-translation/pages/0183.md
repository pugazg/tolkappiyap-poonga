# Blossom 57 — What is a Fortune?

The evening of life portrayed by Tolkāppiyar

Could be compared with the perennial river merging into the sea  
Originating from the hills and flowing through the paddy fields,  
It renders benefits a thousand before reaching the sea.  
Since it writes its epilogue while merging into the sea,  
Nobody would conclude that the story of the river is all at a close.  
Even the lives of the dead continue through their progeny.  
The same is applicable to the river.

Continuing to live even after death is an honour to great people.

The sun is warm at rise and goes scorching at the peak,  
And before vanishing in the west,  
Thanks to the comforting south wind,  
Showers happiness upon the entire earth.

Likewise,  
The aged who are in the last phase of their life,  
Having enjoyed the felicities of conjugal life
