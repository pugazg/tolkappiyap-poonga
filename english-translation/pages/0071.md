# Blossom 17 — Finished Cooking; I have Come

He had invited friends to a feast. Two of the friends arrived even before midnoon. His wife feigned anger, “What dear! You have invited your friends without intimating to me in advance.”

“I bought murrel fish. I would be only too happy to share them with my friends. Since you would also like the gesture, I invited those two friends. Hurry up! Look after the cooking.”

The mistress of the house was activated immediately. She cleaned the rice, put it in boiling water, lighted the stove, chopped the fish into equal pieces, and after mixing the requisite amount of spice, salt, and tamarind, put them one after another into the boiling curry. She kept herself focused upon the food being prepared on both the stoves.

It is indeed a joyful thing for her to serve food to the guests and sit eating with them.

The husband, seated on the veranda of the house, repeatedly asked his wife, “Is the food ready? Why is it taking so long? Why is it taking so long?”
