# Blossom 75 — Four Sources of Wonder

## Novelty (putumai)

Even now, mothers beseech the distant and bewitching moon through songs to come down and play with their children. The moon has never accepted their *invitation and come down.* Even though the moon hasn’t descended to the earth, thanks to the marvels of science, an American astronaut, Armstrong, put his feet on the moon, hoisted his national flag and came back to the earth bringing with him stone-like things from the moon. Isn’t this ‘newness’ a thing of wonder for us?

## Hugeness (perumai)

Gandhiji who went to South Africa as an advocate stood his ground in the struggle against the racism practised there. Transcending from being the ‘Saint of Wardha’ he, through his intelligence, energy and selfless effort, gained in stature and esteem not only in India but also in the international arena. Through his perseverance and relentless practice of non-violence, he secured freedom for his *country, an astonishing feat* indeed, achieved through his hugeness of effort and dedication. The flag of peace hoisted by him flutters still.
