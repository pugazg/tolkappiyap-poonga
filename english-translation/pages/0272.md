# Blossom 98 — Primary and Secondary Works

> Usage is  
> What is in vogue and use  
> Among the literate  
> From whom issue  
> The events of popular speech.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Marapiyal*, verse 93)

> Primary or original works  
> Are those compositions  
> By men of sage wisdom  
> Whose consummate faculties stand unsullied  
> By the mire of turpitude.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Marapiyal*, verse 95)

> Secondary works comprise four modes.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Marapiyal*, verse 97)
