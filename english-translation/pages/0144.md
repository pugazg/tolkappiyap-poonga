# Blossom 43 — Overwhelmed by Love · continued

With telltale evidences he came to know that you are a human lass  
Daughter of the earth, flower-like beauty,  
A woman filled with freshness--  
All these you are though,

Like the grass bending towards the stream’s course  
You are inclined towards love, as if it were your very existence.

You are united as one in emotions and body.”

The companions of the two pile up consolations one after another  
And yet their hearts remain weighed down.  
The confidante of the heroine loses on this score  
While the hero’s friend also doesn’t meet with success.

It is not the failure in a battle fought though,  
Yet they fail in the task of reducing the momentum of love.

The lady remains thinking about the hero all the time.  
What was his predicament?  
The hero with the body of teakwood--  
He stood dispirited with emaciated form afflicted by passion’s extreme.  
Everything seen by the lady reminded her of him  
While the man found her in everything he saw.

Not knowing what they could do,  
The male friend and the maid friend invoked the omnipresent God present in each pillar and particle  
To stand by them and bless them.  
The answer would be “I am in the heart of the man and the heart of woman  
In the form of love  
Though not in the form of the God.”

Lost in love they forget the deed of the day and the inclination for doing too!  
Confusion, bewilderment, forgetting,  
Passions’ urge going to the extreme  
As to drive the partners to suicide  
Such is the intensity of passion *Kaḷaviyal* of *Tolkāppiyam* shows.
