# Blossom 52 — A Woman’s Victory

The little one was moving in the direction of his mother

After having run, played in the water and blown bubbles of foam.  
The anklets he wore and his child’s patter made wonderful music.

Even as he was nearing his mother,  
The other woman in his father’s life stopped him to embrace and kiss him.  
Thinking that her son would reject the gestures and come to her,  
The mother waited along.

Seeing that he didn’t,  
She became angry and exclaimed,  
“The sprout will follow the seed.”  
He my man keeps saying,  
“You’re the elder sister  
While the other women in my life are your younger sisters.  
You’re the genuine pearl while the others are the fake ones.”  
But he goes after the other pearls.
