# Blossom 6 — Production of Speech Sounds - How Admirable!

This is an imaginary scene depicting Paṉampāraṉār credited with composing the prologue to Tolkāppiyam, the great work composed by Tolkāppiyar to preserve the glorious culture of the Tamils. Tolkāppiyar’s work had been presented in the court of the Pāṇṭiya king before the learned bards headed by Ataṅkōṭṭācāṉ.

Even when he was strolling leisurely, the debate in sweet Tamil, which sounded melodious like the music of the *veena* blended with the sea wind, hit his ears. Paṉampāraṉār turned back and stared intently. A few poets, five or six seated in the hall near the beach, were engrossed in a discussion. Upon seeing him, they stood up in obeisance and welcomed him.

“What is the discussion about? Is there any debate?” Enquiring thus, Paṉampāraṉār sat among them in the hall.

A poet replied, “There is no debate as such. We were just talking about the doubt arising from the *Eḻuttatikāram* section of Tolkāppiyar’s *Tolkāppiyam*, presented recently in the royal court.”

“What is that doubt?” questioned Paṉampāraṉār.

“In the section describing the articulation of the sounds of letters under the title ‘Production of Speech Sounds’, Tolkāppiyar has stated scientifically that each class of letters possesses specific characteristics”, a poet said at the outset.
