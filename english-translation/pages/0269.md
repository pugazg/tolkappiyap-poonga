# Blossom 97 — Those Who Realize will Know, Those Who Know will Realize · continued

> Can we throw it away?  
> Good is it to hand over this rose beautiful,  
> To a monkey wild?  
> Our freedom let us zealously guard,  
> Let us add glory to those freedom fighters.

Thus is the age of new poetry, an age when novelties like haiku are being composed.

To put it in other words, it is the age when words and sentences are juggled or lines broken and they are paraded as rhythmic poetry.

I do not claim that I am the one versed in prosody, and yet my verse portraits are no eyeless needles. I have the sense of pride to proclaim that my poems have remained the magnetic particles drawing even eyeless needles unto their fold. Here are a few examples:

> The world to be one with all lands united,  
> Mankind to be knit as one by human love-  
> Are the noble thoughts articulated by the poet.
>
> But even he, he as a poet did live  
> With his place name, Pūṅkuṉṟam, after his name.  
> And he was known as Kaṇiyaṉ Pūṅkuṉṟaṉār.
>
> When we contemplate this carefully  
> Doesn’t it strike as true  
> That even this man of eminence  
> Was bound by the love of his land?

Another poem:

> He was the lion of a Tamil with self-respect  
> Who wouldn’t bend  
> Even to kiss his toddling child;  
> Instead he would get the child lifted over his head  
> And go about fondling it.
>
> He was not used to looking down to the ground,  
> And he would plant a kiss  
> On the forehead of his love of moon-like face  
> With the face straight up.
