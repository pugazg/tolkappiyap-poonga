# Blossom 32 — Warriors who Protected Honour and Valour

The *Puṟattiṇai* chapter in *Tolkāppiyam* speaks of seven thematic situations (*tiṇais*) of *Puṟam* corresponding to the seven *Akam* situations. They are

1. *Veṭci* (counterpart of *kuṟiñci*) is the seizure of the cattle of an enemy king in secret by the vassals or officers of his rival (and taking the animals under the latter’s protection), an act provoking war in ancient times. The counter-attack in the form of fighting and recovery of the seized cattle by the enemy king’s men is also part of this *tiṇai*.

2. *Vañci* (counterpart of *mullai*) is the march of one king with his forces against another, causing fear to the subjects of the kingdoms; it is motivated by the ambition of seizing territory through conquest.

3. *Uḻiñai* (counterpart of *marutam*) is the siege by an attacking king of a fort, along with its defence by the king remaining within.
