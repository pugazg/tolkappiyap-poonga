# Blossom 73 — Four Sources of Grief

## Contempt (iḷivu)

Socrates, the thinker par excellence, stands trial in the Greek court of law. The charge against him is that he instigated the youth against the state by his speeches. A group of three persons raise a furore proposing and seconding those charges in the court. These three persons-Anytus, Lichen and Meletus-level charges against Socrates which are in the nature of contempt of the great thinker.

**Anytus:** *Seething volcano! Turbulent ocean! More terrible than all these is Socrates! ... Weapon of Wisdom! All the world would surrender to this missile, it is said!... If that weapon were to be manufactured in Greece, we would not be able to raise our heads, Meletus! What do you say, Lichen?*

**Lichen:** I am a sculptor who carves God out of stone...! Once even this Socrates was a sculptor like me! But now he has metamorphosed into a sculptor of *thoughts! It’s all right! After the three of us - me who create God out of stones,* Meletus the poet who with his Midas touch gives divine shapes to bundles of imagination, and a political genius like you - once we have colluded together..., Socrates is nothing more than a worthless particle upon an ocean, isn’t he, Anytus...!
