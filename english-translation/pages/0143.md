# Blossom 43 — Overwhelmed by Love

He, lost in the thoughts of the beauty met by the Amman shrine,  
And she, carrying the image of him in her heart’s depths,  
Are looking forward to meeting again  
In anxious expectation penance-like.

They won’t sleep, nor would they feel hungry,  
Like chilli powder sprinkled over the wound  
Their hearts pained,  
Which they suppressed by lips held tight together.

For how many days would they keep it undisclosed  
Without the knowledge of parents?

Would lightning get concealed in darkness?  
Or would darkness ban lightning tearing through it?

Just as the hero has a confidant right,  
The heroine also has a beautiful maid for her companion.

“He was startled as to whether you were a goddess;  
You called him a thief!
