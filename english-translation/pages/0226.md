# Blossom 76 — Four Sources of Fear · continued

## Thieves (kaḷvar)

Laymen as well as the learned are naturally subjected to fear on either hearing about or encountering thieves, groups of robbers or highwaymen.

## Grievous Wrong (iṟai)

In this list of things evoking fear, wrong deeds of a serious nature are also included. Many commentators state that *iṟai* refers to the king. If *iṟai* is not taken in isolation and read together with the prefix as *tam iṟai* it becomes *tammiṟai*, which means ‘one’s wrong deeds of a grievous nature’. It is quite natural that such wrong-doers dread punishment Veḷḷaivāraṇar also points to the double entendre involved in *tam iṟai* and *tammiṟai*.

* * *

> The four sources of unmitigated fear are  
> Demoniac beings, wild animals, thieves and depravity.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 8)
