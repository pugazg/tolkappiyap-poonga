# Blossom 20 — Leadership Traits · continued

Just as *mullai* land is inhabited by shepherds and hunters, the other types of land also have farmers, etc. engaged in occupations characteristic of those geographical divisions:

> Cowherds and huntsmen  
> Designate region-specific persons of the masculine gender;  
> There are other names of principal characters such as these two  
> Drawn that they are on the region’s occupation.

> We find on reflection that  
> Inhabitants of other regions too  
> Have their names derived from the mores of their region  
> And from the occupation of that region.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verses 21, 22)

> Such representation of *kaikkiḷai* and *peruntiṇai*  
> Does mark men of other menial occupations too.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse 24)

Tolkāppiyar does not go by the social status, and assigns the position of the hero and heroine even to those folks engaged in lowly occupations.

There are, however, some scholars amongst us who tend to misinterpret *ēval marapu* (menial work) as that of ordering others.
