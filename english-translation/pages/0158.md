# Blossom 47 — Wedded Love · continued

From the line ‘the three monarchs of benign note’ (*Ceyyuḷiyal*, 75), it is clear that ‘the upper three’ is the trinity of ancient Tamil kings, which gives us to see that the ancient Tamils classified people corresponding to the fourfold division of land and not in terms of fourfold caste division. The lower section then refers to the subject people of the three monarchs.

- Veḷḷaivāraṇar
