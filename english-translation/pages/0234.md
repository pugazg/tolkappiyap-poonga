# Blossom 81 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Second Stage of Physical Manifestations

The physical manifestations of the first stage of Akam love  
Consist of the dilemma as to who would initiate the act;  
She wipes off the beads of sweat on the forehead,  
Unties the tresses of her hair which add to the beauty of her person,  
And then ties them up immediately.

Her body seems emaciated in no moment owing to the passion of love.  
Pretending to have lost an ear-stud,  
She walks the street and the garden under the pretext of looking for it.  
She gropes for the ring on the finger and bangles on the wrist
