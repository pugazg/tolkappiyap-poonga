# Blossom 49 — Clandestine Love Career and Wedded Course

*Tolkāppiyam* shows us that

*Karaṇam* (marriage) is  
The hero taking the heroine’s hand in marriage  
As she is offered by those of a line worthy and compatible  
With the rites and ceremonies attendant thereon.

He also shows that  
It is marriage  
As the lovers take to elopement  
Without the heroine’s parents offering her hand.

In both the events  
The lovers enter into wedded course
