# Blossom 5 — Ethnic Unity

It was a glorious hall, adorned with the portraits of illustrious Tamil kings, renowned poets and filled with audience eager to taste the manna of Tamil discourse. Vaḷavaṉ, a Tamil scholar and an intellectual with a broad forehead, penetrating eyes, slightly grey sprouting beard and blessed with ideas like an ever-flowing river, was lecturing on the title ‘Race’ (*iṉam*).*

The origin of various races in the world, the way they prospered, and the interaction and conflict between them were detailed with precision and historical evidences along with the researchers’ unambiguous views. Vaḷavaṉ’s discourse proved to be a feast as well as a medicine for the audience. “Now let me explain the rise and fall of our Tamil race”, announced Vaḷavaṉ after stealing a glance at the audience who were unconsciously drooping their heads like a bunch of lotus flowers in stooping posture due to the weight of their own petals.

Vaḷavaṉ spoke, “The Tamilians are one race, but Tamil itself constitutes three races. The speakers of the language form one race. How then is their language divided into three races?

Yes, gentlemen, what this humble man of yours states is an irrefutable truth! In our Tamil language the consonants could be divided into three races (categories):

*k, c, ṭ, t, p, ṟ* --> are *vallinam* (hard consonants)

*ṅ, ñ, ṇ, n, m, ṉ* --> are *mellinam* (soft consonants)

*y, r, l, v, ḻ, ḷ* --> are *iṭaiyiṉam* (medial consonants)

*The Tamil word iṉam, among others, means race, and a section or class of something. Here, the author plays pun on the meanings of this word.
