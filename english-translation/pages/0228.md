# Blossom 77 — Four Sources of Pride · continued

## Munificence (koṭai)

It is the Koṅku land  
Where  
The women wear smiles bud-like  
That are sweet like mellifluous Tamiḻ  
Mixed with honey bees collect from flowers,  
And walk with a sense of pride  
Saying, “Even the tiny particles of food  
Carried by ants  
Are gifts from our king  
Kumaṇaṉ the munificent.”

Such is the emotion of pride as arising from the feeling that Kumaṇaṉ’s name is synonymous with liberality.

* * *

> The four sources of rightful pride are  
> Learning, prowess, unsullied fame and munificence absolute.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 9)
