# Blossom 45 — Shyness or Honour?

“I am returning after seeing the lover”, said the heroine.

“I understood that much-after seeing your dishevelled hair”,  
Was the quick reply of the confidante.

“What happened to cause such things disorderly in your form, dear?  
Tell me how your mind has its composure lost.”

For the confidante to see  
The heroine bared her shoulder.

Bloodshot marks!  
Signs of scratches like the crescent moon!  
Even when the bruises healed, the scars deep remained intact.

“My God! How bad!  
For heaven’s sake, don’t think I am insulting your hero;  
Does he belong to the species of animals or human beings?  
You got trapped by him;  
It would have been better even if he had gobbled you.  
But why did you permit him to maul you in frenzy of lust?”
