# Blossom 7 — Intrusive Consonant

She was an exquisite beauty, full moon of the month of Cittirai. Her curvaceous form could break even the celibacy vows of Lord Siva. She was a blossom by age but her body was not past a bud.

She stood before a cascade and her eyes lusted upon the well-built figure of a handsome male bathing nude. Unaware of her presence, he bathed nude in the cascade which changed her flustering to a flare-up of passion.

Without bothering about her neat dress, she pounced upon the bathing man to his dismay. “My dear, why are you quivering?” The cheeky girl enquired and made her mark upon his lips.

Pushing her away, he gathered sense to wrap up the towel lying on the bank. “What did you do?” He enquired angrily.

“What wrong did I do? I was just practising the lesson learnt from the teacher!”

“Look, girl! I might be your teacher; but I am not the poet Utāraṉ of Pilkaṇīyam, nor are you Amutavalli whom Utāraṉ taught.”

“My Lord! Our names might have changed, but who would prevent us from playing the roles of Amutavalli and Utāraṉ?”

She spoke thus and embraced him again.

“Fie upon you! Leave me! Is this the way a student behaves with a teacher? Are you not ashamed?”
