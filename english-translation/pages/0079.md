# Blossom 21 — Land and People: A Study in Excellence

We get to know that Tolkāppiyar designates land by the term world, which points to his universal perspective to human existence, though it conspicuously lacked individual features related to the Tamil race.

In spite of specific characteristics and vocations allotted to each type of land, there are overlappings with regard to certain features. But in general, the characteristics pertain to the type of land mentioned.

Each type of land has people bearing two categories of names, viz., conventional names and occupation-related names:

> The persons that people a tract of land  
> Have names specific to that region;  
> These names fall into two categories:  
> Names in tune with the mores of the region  
> And names derived from occupations of that region.  
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse 20)
