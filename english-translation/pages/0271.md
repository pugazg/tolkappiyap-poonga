# Blossom 97 — Those Who Realize will Know, Those Who Know will Realize · continued

> He is now the ruler of fame winning.  
> Look not at my eyes raining tears!  
> Look at my heart in joy immersed!

One more of my poems:

> Such a joy she did not experience on her marriage;  
> Son’s birth too did not give her as much happiness.
>
> Look! there he lies dead  
> With a spear piercing his chest!  
> She saw this and her heart leapt in joy.
>
> O, I was about to pluck away my breasts that fed him  
> O, where is that wicked man who told me a blatant lie  
> (That he died with a spear on his back)?  
> Here is the sword! Where is the tongue of that man?

I keep dedicating at the feet of Mother Tamil such of these poetic portraits that are a blend of the sound of the sword’s sway and the music of the harp, and that have infused my fellow Tamils with a new vigour and a sense of amazement.

I might have strayed away from the established conventions of versification. But my mind has all along remained undistracted. The readers who have had a feel of the import of the blossoms of Tolkāppiyam presented so far by me would understand this truth. And those who have understood this will feel it in their heart of hearts.
