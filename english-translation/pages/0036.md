# Blossom 3 — Consonant-vowels

I was enjoying the pleasant caressing of breeze, sitting on the beach.

The darkness of night was slowly swallowing the dusk, when a friend said, “Oh! The lights are already on,” forcing me to wake up from the trance.

Sitting near Gandhiji’s statue, I turned towards the northern direction to look at Aṇṇā’s statue and my mind raced back in memory lane.

The electrical poles erected near Aṇṇā’s statue were spilling light over me. My poetic elegy on the All India Radio on the eve of Aṇṇā’s demise rushed back to my memory. Or rather, I am sorry; when did I forget it for me to remember it?

> We looked up to you as our shade of life,  
> but you went in search of solace in the land off the sea.  
> Is this fair?  
> Establishing your supremacy among the  
> pearls underneath sea,  
> You sleep peacefully on the beach!
>
> Your tongue had mothered  
> Many a speech sweet like nākaswaram’s strain;  
> Why is it tied up now?  
> You wished to possess a heart  
> which could weather any shock.
