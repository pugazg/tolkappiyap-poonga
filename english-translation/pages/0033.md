# Blossom 1 — Letters of the Alphabet · continued

***

> The secondary phonemes are  
> The shortened /i/ (*kuṟṟiyalikaram*), the shortened /u/ (*kuṟṟiyalukaram*),  
> And the three-dotted āytam [ஃ],  
> Which too partake of a phoneme’s nature.
>
> (*Nūl Marapu*, verse 2)
