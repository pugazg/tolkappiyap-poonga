# Blossom 19 — Land and Time

Given that the types and modes of love behaviour of the hero and the heroine and the attendant situations and sub-situations as prescribed in *Tolkāppiyam* are being discussed in the succeeding chapters, it is necessary to briefly introduce them here.

Pastoral tract (*mullai*): Season--rainy season; time of the day - evening.

Hilly tract (*kuṟiñci*): Season--winter; time of the day - midnight

Agricultural tract (*marutam*): Season--all seasons; time of the day - dawn

Maritime land (*neytal*): Season--all seasons; time of the day - sunset.

The desert tract, not included among the four above, has the hot summer season and the midday hour for its season and hour of the day.

> The rainy season and evening  
> Are assigned to the forest tract.  
> The learned find  
> The cold season and midnight hour  
> Assigned to the hilly tract.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse 6)

> The early dewy season  
> Does also go with the hilly tract,  
> So has it been said.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse7)
