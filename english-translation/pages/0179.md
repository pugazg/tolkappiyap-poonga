# Blossom 54 — The Warrior on the Field

(*Poruḷatikāram : Karpiyal*, verse 50)

When the elephants trample their way through the field  
Much like the mountains rolling down,  
The hero remains unperturbed and takes them head on.

He would fight to keep his country’s flag aloft,  
Even if he were to lose his life in the process!

The heroine had applied the *tilak* of blood on his forehead  
Bidding farewell and wishing him victory.

In the thick of the battle he does not even think about her.  
Only after his country’s flag is unfurled on the enemy’s fortress  
Would the chaste warrior send the message  
By the breeze blowing across the countries:  
“My dear damsel! Victory is ours!”  
This is yet another portrait of ancient Tamiḻ life in *Tolkāppiyam*.

***

> No reflection of the heroine at home  
> Finds utterance from the hero away on his mission;  
> The task accomplished,  
> He wears her plight upon his sleeve.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Karpiyal*, verse 45)
