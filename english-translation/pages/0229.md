# Blossom 78 — Four Sources of Anger

In a scenario where anybody wielding a stick becomes a ruler, a reign of terror is unleashed--like chopping people’s heads through violence and suppression, vicious acts of severing hands and legs, refusal to even appreciate the cause of opposition and mercilessly trampling them down with spouse and children, incessant beating and acts of terrorism with the approval of the government, murder committed without anybody raising even a muffled protest or making efforts to prevent it, with the people writhing like worms in a fire, unable to bear the injustice--where people rise like tigers. This transformation is what is called anger.

* * *

> The four sources of hate-born anger are  
> Cutting one’s limbs, harming one’s kin,  
> Inflicting pain and anguish and ‘killing’.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 10)
