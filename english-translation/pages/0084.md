# Blossom 23 — Women and Palm Leaves

Mukilaṉ approached Mullaikkoṭi, when she was coming from the classroom with hands full of the books, received as a prize.

“I am so happy! Congratulations on your winning the prize!” Mukilaṉ said with a smile on his face but he made no attempt to hide his anger.

“Mukilaṉ! Help me with these books. I am unable to carry them.”

“Oh! Do you take me for an aide?” So saying, Mukilaṉ took the bundle of books she received as a prize.

“What Mullai? What sort of a feminist are you? You were advocating male chauvinism!”

“What, me advocating male chauvinism?”

“Exactly! Didn’t you argue in favour of *Tolkāppiyam* prohibiting women from accompanying men on voyage?”

“Who said that it was *Tolkāppiyam*’s idea? In the ancient era, such a practice was in vogue. Tolkāppiyar merely reflected it, without concealing anything.”

“I didn’t want to subscribe to that retrogressive principle, which is why I didn’t mention it. But you managed to receive appreciation by holding up views against your own gender. A worst form of female subjugation! Is this how *Tolkāppiyam* depicts women?”
