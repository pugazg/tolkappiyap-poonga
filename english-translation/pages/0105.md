# Blossom 31 — Peruntiṇai · continued

The concept of *peruntiṇai* then is an invaluable treasure in so far as it serves as a warning against such deeds.

Tolkāppiyar has chosen to treat this concept as a thing to be excluded.

***

The four defining features of abnormal sexual passion (*peruntiṇai*) are:

> Taking to riding of the horse made of palmyra stems;  
> Man and woman past their youth drawn to each other;  
> The passion of lust growing uncontained and out of proportion;  
> And excessive lust seeking gratification through forced union.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse 51).
