# Blossom 71 — Manifest Emotions Explained Further · continued

The *Tolkāppiyam* verse in this regard occurs as the first verse of the *Meyppāṭṭiyal* section:

> As the literate men say,  
> Thirty-two are the sources  
> That represent spheres of human sport  
> Wherein are born the eight emotions  
> With each emotion having four sources.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 1)

There are thirty-two sources of human sport wherein are born things sixteen when classified.

Music and dance enjoyed in those days by the three crowned monarchs and the chieftains, the emotions that they experienced as they witnessed these ‘sports’ (*paṇṇai*), the objects of these emotions (*cuvaippaṭuporuḷ*), the inner responses they felt (*kuṟippu*) and their external manifestations (*viṟal*) - these four expand into thirty-two as is seen in the *Tolkāppiyam* verse above.
