# Blossom 38 — The Light amidst the Darkness

Even as the Aryan culture had come to entrench itself on the Tamil soil as a tiny wart on a lovely face though refusing to go off except through a surgery, the *Puṟam* theme of *vākai* reflects an admirable dimension of the native cultural mores of the ancient Tamils.

Be it winter or summer, in war-camps there are the warriors and their generals closeted together working out the strategies of war and remaining ever alert for eventualities of martial campaigns.

The peasants who provide for others’ sustenance through their labour get into joyous rapture on witnessing bumper crops.

The enemy routed by acts of valour, the king in ecstasy joins his soldiers in their *kuravai* dance.

Female dancers present a feast of dance by teaming up with the soldiers.

The great heroes, who offered to sacrifice their lives on the warfield where occasion warranted, are seen inundated in the shower of adulation.

The men of humanitarian propensities demonstrate their love even to their enemies.
