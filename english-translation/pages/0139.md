# Blossom 41 — That Angel - Who Is She? · continued

From her hair flowing down  
And as only the flowers sported by the humans  
Emit fragrance,  
It dawned upon him that she was no goddess.

He looked at the ornaments on the body  
And saw that they were all the ones made by the goldsmith of this world.

Having finished his study on half of her body,  
He was in raptures understanding that she was not a goddess.  
The drawings on her breasts and shoulders seen,  
And the fragrance of lily flower held by her smelt,  
And known for certain  
That the eyes on her rounded moon-like face  
Were but the human eyes all flesh and blood,  
Also clearing his mind that  
If she were an angel, her eyelids would not twitch like this  
And seeing that she was perturbed after noticing him, a man,  
It was all obvious that  
She was not an angel but a pigeon  
Who came searching for him.

The fact of her being a human fully dawned on him.  
And the prankster pal was embarrassed and hid himself near the bush  
Realizing the emerging scene of his friend and the lady united.

***

> The swarming bees, the ornaments worn,  
> The ornamental lines drawn on the body,  
> The fading flowers, human eye, signs of disquietude  
> The winking of eyes and timidity  
> And such of these that mark the person  
> There manifest for the doubt to vanish.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Kaḷaviyal*, verse 4)
