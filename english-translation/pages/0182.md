# Blossom 56 — The Horse and the Bird would Stand Outsmarted! · continued

The horse should understand that and gallop towards its destination  
Without taking a break in between,  
Without any hindrances on the way home.  
Vēḷaṉ spurred the horse on;  
The horse sped like a bird’s flight;  
And it was the bird  
From which he took the cue for his flight home.

When Tolkāppiyar says  
The horse matched the speed of the bird,  
We cannot help entertaining a doubt.

The bird flying in the sky should rapidly flutter its wing to fly fast,  
And the horse has to gallop to reach home,  
Both of which might take quite some time.

But Vēḷaṉ’s heart  
Burning with passion intense for his lady  
Could be home to her  
The moment he thought he should be home.

***

> No sojourn does the hero’s journey across the parched tract mark  
> As he is homeward after his mission accomplished;  
> For he had at his possession  
> A sturdy horse swift as a bird  
> That abides at his service  
> As does his benignant heart.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Karpiyal*, verse 53)
