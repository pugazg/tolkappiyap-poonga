# Blossom 67 — The Truth Unknown to the Devotee · continued

On the way, the guest enquired, “Is it vegetarian food or non-vegetarian?”

“How come you have such a doubt? Today is the day of Lord Siva--*Sivarāttiri*. God is Love. How can you forget all these and ask me such a question?”

“It is okay! In any case it is better to clear the doubt in advance. That is exactly why I enquired.”

“Okay, sir, you need not doubt at all. Dishes made of brinjal, drumstick, unripe banana and other such vegetables combined with rice of excellent quality await you. Please eat to your heart’s content.”

“Let it be so”.

“Hara Hara Siva Siva, Sambo Mahadeva! Let me squat on the veranda. Please invite me after the food gets ready.” The guest lounged on the veranda of the house muttering Siva, Siva. A few moments might have passed, when a voice assailed the ears of the guest,

*Iṟaicci* (meaning ‘meat’ among others)! *Iṟaicci!* though the explanation that followed was barely audible.

“What is this? A queer conversation is ensuing.” The guest became apprehensive.

Again the word *iṟaicci* loudly uttered fell on his ears.

The guest peeped inside the house.

Again the matter related to *iṟaicci* reared its head though the context remained unclear.

It was time for food--but the persistent sound inside was, *iṟaicci, iṟaicci, iṟaicci!*

The startled guest got up abruptly.

“How dare he cheats me after inviting me for a vegetarian food;” He raged, and left the place in a huff and doubled up.

What had happened actually?

The host in his anxiety to look for a devotee as a guest left his teaching of a chapter of *Tolkāppiyam* to his son incomplete.

Then the host was engrossed in clearing the doubts of his son.

> *Iṟaicci* is  
> The suggestive import  
> Paralleling the Akam thematic strand explicit.
>
> That from *iṟaicci*  
> There emerges an implied subtext  
> Beside its evocative designation of flora and fauna  
> Will be obvious to men of discernment.
