# Blossom 62 — Do Speak, We would Listen...

Is the word *ilakkiyam* of Tamil origin? Or does it belong to the Sanskrit language? This was the topic of a big debate.

This issue necessitated more or less a forum for debate. It came up in the function on *Muttamiḻ* (Threefold Tamil) organised in May 1949 at Coimbatore. Pāvēntar Pāratitācaṉ presided over the function. I was also present at the function in which Ariñar Aṇṇā, Avvai Duraisamy Piḷḷai, the multilingual scholar Appadurai, the great critic Veḷḷaivāraṇar and many others participated.

Hardly was there any discourse on the development of Tamil or the rise of the Tamils.

In the huge gathering of ten thousand people, the only question that dominated the proceedings was ‘Is *ilakkiyam* a Tamil word or not?’

Speaking on the second day, I observed thus:

“Having mobilized ten thousand people and spent thousands of rupees to conduct this function to which renowned scholars and academics are invited, you have wasted two days’ time and deliberations by focusing upon the single issue of deciding as to whether *ilakkiyam* is a Tamil word or not.
