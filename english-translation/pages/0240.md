# Blossom 84 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Fifth Stage of Physical Manifestations

The clandestine course on  
With the consent of the heroine,  
And in tune with the convention of  
The heroine’s acceptance of the hero’s gift of love.  
She takes the gift of the hero with passion,  
Hands it to her parents submissively  
And looks for their consent to marriage.  
“You have brought disgrace to our clan!  
We have become the object of ridicule of folks around!  
People laugh at us everywhere!
