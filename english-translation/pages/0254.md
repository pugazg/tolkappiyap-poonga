# Blossom 91 — Metaphor or Simile?

> Where the tenor is made the vehicle  
> [And the vehicle the tenor],  
> It is the mode of comparison proper  
> Insofar as the vehicle-turned tenor  
> Abides as one of a loftier note.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Uvamaiyiyal*, verse 9)

It is clear from this verse that even though the subject of comparison is represented in the form of a simile, the simile stands out unambiguously, given the fact that the simile-turned subject is shown as one of heightened estimation.

Pērāciriyar, a commentator, quotes the following *Cirupāṇāṟṟuppaṭai* lines and explains it thus:

> The solid bud  
> Lovely face-like,  
> Resembling the maturing breast  
> Breaking open,  
> There unfolds  
> The divine lotus.
