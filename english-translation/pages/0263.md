# Blossom 95 — The Cultural Programme and the Gift of Kaḷiru

> The male of the elephant is kaḷiṟu.  
> Kaḷiṟu denoting the male pig  
> Is not disallowed too.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Marapiyal*, verses 35, 36)

> Kalai is the male of the deer too;  
> Kalai is also the male of the langur (mucu).

(*Poruḷatikāram : Marapiyal*, verse 47)
