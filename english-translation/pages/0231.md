# Blossom 79 — Four Sources of Happiness · continued

We shall shout, “Let milk (*pāl*) overflow!”  
That *Pāl* is the one Vaḷḷuvar has gifted us—  
The *Pāls* of *Aṟam, Poruḷ* and *Kāmam*.  
What more do we need?

Let’s cleanse our homes, cows, calves and get rid of dust and dirt.  
This is the real Bhogi!

Let’s uproot superstitious beliefs and remove their stench,  
Chisel our grey cells and rational thinking and gather knowledge.  
Let’s twist our moustaches  
And work for the empowerment of our womenfolk.

Let our women’s lips wear buds of smile -  
The women who love turmeric, ginger and sugar cane lofty  
And lands fertile!

Bull-fight for our youth was a child’s play then.  
Righteousness and fair-play are essential in public life.  
The joy of such a new life will cross the boundaries of the earth.  
Hail *Poṅkal!*

* * *

> The four sources of unmixed joy are  
> Riches, mind’s might, sexual union and play.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 11)
