# Blossom 22 — Reward for Objection

The Tamiḻ professor made an announcement in the classroom of the college: “Students, I announce a competition to commemorate the birth anniversary of the Tamiḻ scholar Thiru V. Ka. The best among you who scene scripts a characterizing a man and a woman in a beautiful manner, in sweet, charming Tamiḻ by conforming to the established rules of old Tamiḻ tradition and history would bag a prize of literature books worth rupees five thousand. Well then! You can as well start now and read out your work to me before the end of this class”.

The students welcomed this announcement and raced against each other to write the script in order to bag the prize.

“There is yet another condition for winning the competition! You should finish it first, and further your work should be prize-worthy.”

Within an hour of the announcement, Mukilaṉ finished his work and he approached the professor with the papers. He said, “Sir, I have finished writing.”

“Is that so! I am quite happy! Why don’t you read it aloud to others?”

Student Mukilaṉ started rushing through with his presentation:

The lover sits by his beloved wearing a sad countenance.

She enquires, “Why are you sorrow-worn? Why does your face look withered?”

“Sulking”, is the crisp reply.

“Sulking? What does it mean...?”
