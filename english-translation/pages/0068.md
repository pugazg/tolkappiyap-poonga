# Blossom 16 — Female Son

Perched beside the greenish bushes of the garden, Marakatam and Taṅkam were discussing sweet nothings. “You, Taṅkam, do you know the scoop! It is widely believed that our Aṉṉam is mentally disturbed, of late.”

“Why? Is there any heartbreak due to love failure? As far as I know, she has not fallen for anybody and none is in love with her, either.”

“I also suspected on the same lines and paid her a visit yesterday.”

“You saw her in person? How is she? Is she blabbering anything?”

“Yes. I saw her in person! She was in the midst of a junk of books!”

“Junk of books? Or useless books?”

“Aṉṉam is never known to have even touched them! She completely abhors such books and for that matter, even odd perverted stories published in weeklies. She discards them, even if they are offered by somebody.”

“Let it be so. Though men and women feign to dislike them, they never miss any opportunity to read them, unnoticed by others. Okay, Marakatam! Aṉṉam always remains engrossed in literature. What has happened to her now?”

“That is why she is afflicted with literature craze.”

“You are also a connoisseur of literature,”

“Not to such an extent as to confuse myself and others too.”

“Please tell me, what is the matter?”

“A bisexual deprived of manliness should not be referred to using musculine verbal suffixes; we should, according to norms of grammar, use feminine suffixes.”

“That is correct! So what? What does Aṉṉam say?”
