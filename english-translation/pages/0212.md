# Blossom 70 — The Eight Emotions · continued

I say  
This section on sentiments does really transport us to ecstasy.

Laughter is what emerges when we obstinately work at a person given to fear.  
The valiant are astonished at people who are not infuriated by humiliation.  
Happiness and weeping are the children of the same mother.

> For us to be served to the fill  
> These  
> Eight sentiments  
> In Tamil  
> Like curd delicious,  
> We shall further see  
> In the ‘blossoms’ succeeding  
> The way Tolkāppiyar,  
> Who was hailed  
> By poets of threefold Tamil  
> In the courts of  
> The crowned monarchs  
> Where fluttered  
> The silken banner  
> Aloft the royal canopy,  
> Looks at them.

* * *

> These eight kinds of manifest emotions are  
> Laughter, grief, disgust, wonder  
> Fear, pride, anger and joy.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 3)
