# Blossom 48 — The Aiyar Conceived the Marriage

The well-meaning elders of the village  
Assembled at the public hall  
And had a brainstorming session:

“I see that youth quite often  
By the pond, near the river bank,  
In the sandy plain where horses fall over.

The young woman keeps waiting for the youth  
Without fail  
Even if the scabbard fish are not to be seen in the river,  
Even if the carp forget to pop up in the pond  
And even if the horses fail to make their visit.

Day in, day out,  
These two frequent under cover  
By the hill, off the home
