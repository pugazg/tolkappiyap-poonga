# Blossom 50 — The Light of Home · continued

A pointed finger was her reply to his concern.  
“Stop there! Are you threatening me?”  
Said the man in mocking jest  
“It has been a month since the period stopped”, said she.

With a joyous roar of laughter  
He took her in his clasp  
Her resistance notwithstanding.

“Mind your health, my dear!  
Conceived, you need to take much care,”  
Cautioning her thus,  
He kept moving behind her all through  
From month one to the ninth month of her pregnancy  
With care and concern undiminished.

He parted from her with a sigh  
As the midwife came to attend to childbirth.

As he heard the words ‘male child’ from the labour room  
He went about dancing in rapture.

Allowed to see his wife, he went in  
And all her pain vanished without a trace.  
The flower of that woman wore a smile  
Holding the bud of the baby close to her.

He wiped the sweat on her forehead,  
Saying, “You are reduced much like a twig, dear!”  
She blushed for a while  
And said with a streak of ridicule at the corner of her eyes:  
“Reduced in just one day’s pain?”

She was showered in benediction by all kindred folk,  
Her parents and foster mother in particular.  
All kindly people thronged to greet her.

She was bathed in oil.  
There was a celebration for the child
