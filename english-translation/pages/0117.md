# Blossom 35 — That Golden Era · continued

> Into the town of the besieged;  
> Triumphant scaling of the fort within;  
> The ceremonial bath of the victorious king  
> And his taking the crown of the vanquished;  
> The ceremonial bath of the sword of the victor;  
> And the absolute surrender of the vanquished.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Puṟattiṇai iyal*, verse 13)
