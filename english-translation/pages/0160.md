# Blossom 48 — The Aiyar Conceived the Marriage · continued

In the jasmine garden, in the coconut grove,  
In the mango grove, in the teakwood jungle;  
They walk hand in hand tiptoe like shadows  
Speaking in hushed tones,  
Deceiving themselves  
That they do it all unknown to the village folk.

We must catch them red-handed--  
The boy who comes furtively  
And the maid of undefiled purity--  
And put an end to their clandestine love career.  
We must see them man and wife married.

The boy might desert the girl  
Despite his promise to her  
That he would forget her not  
Even if it be death to him.

A time may come  
When falsehood and deception would rule the day;  
We cannot afford to lose the girl  
And go about crying over it.

We must talk to them right today;  
We must take the matter to their parents.”

As the elders were splitting their heads thus,  
The lovers made their presence right before them.

The headman stately as the temple car addressed them:  
“See how worried the folks of the hamlet are!  
You keep meeting in rendezvous secret  
By day and by night,  
Near the house, by the fence  
And everywhere around.

How would you convince us  
That you would not desert the girl eventually?”  
Fire of rage flew from the headman’s eyes.
