# Blossom 25 — That is like a Mother!

Love is a ticklish phenomenon which binds the lovers together but prohibits union and attainment of ecstasy. A girl entangled in love is sleeping beside her mother. Her mother’s hand, like a creeper swept by the breeze, is moving around her body, making the girl realize that she is still wide awake.

It is the winter season, specific to the *mullai* land. The time of the day is midnight. The prayer song sung to appease the presiding deity Kaṇṇaṉ, echoing itself in the hills and trees of the forest, hits the ears of the mother and the daughter, though the actual song ended long before.

All of a sudden, the girl wakes up and sits. The mother without revealing that she is awake turns to the other side. The daughter prays, “Kaṇṇa! Thanks for your mercy! Just as I wished, he has agreed to forget me. On my part, I will not think of him either.”

A satisfied mother goes to sleep with happiness thinking that the *mullai* deity Kaṇṇaṉ has changed the minds of the lovers.

Realizing that her strategy has succeeded, the girl pretends to sleep for some time, only to nimble-footedly walk her way to the handsome man hiding behind the tree outside. She touches his shoulders and both of them set out on the journey.

The cock which heralds the breaking of day seems to tell tales of elopement of the lover and his condescending lady, without parents’ knowledge, making a startled mother wake up, and realize the disaster. The friend of the girl, who abets the elopement, joins the mother in her grief.
