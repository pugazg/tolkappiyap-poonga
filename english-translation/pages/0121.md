# Blossom 36 — Persistence in War like an Unyielding Buffalo · continued

> The famed act of a warrior  
> Who finds his king felled in action  
> And rises in ire uncontained to fight the foe  
> And the feat exceptional of the warrior  
> That sends columns of enemy’s army into disarray  
> And goes about waving his brilliant winning sword.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Puṟattiṇai iyal*, verse 19)
