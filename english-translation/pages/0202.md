# Blossom 66 — Greatness and Humility

The land throbs with valour,  
The eyes of women express chastity.

The shadow of the rising flag would conquer all enemies.  
The anthems are pregnant with pride.

The fort of warriors is teeming with myriad talents.  
The golden walls of their fort have the silvery clouds for their canopies.

The sea which kisses the walls of the fort is in penance,  
The people of this land hold not life too dear.

Behold Rāvaṇa, the one with eternal fame!  
The beacon light of Lanka,  
The flame of self-esteem,  
The leader of the great Tamil race,  
For whom protecting the subjects is the norm of the reign.
