# Blossom 10 — Alliance of Letters · continued

That the three letters, *kuṟṟiyalikaram, kuṟṟiyalukaram* and *āytam*, are in a similar condition is true.

Caught in a piquant situation, wherein they are dependent upon and possess no individuality, the three letters - *kuṟṟiyalikaram /i/, kuṟṟiyalukaram /u/, āytam /ḵ/* take the origin of letters upon which they are dependent as their own origin, thereby functioning as secondary letters.

***

> The three secondary phonemes  
> [Shortened /i/, shortened /u/ and /ḵ/]  
> Discerned to be dependent and sans distinctiveness,  
> Have for their places of articulation  
> The organs of their respective primary phonemes;  
> And yet speaking relatively,  
> They are marked by natures of their own.
>
> (*Eḻuttatikāram : Piṟappiyal*, verse 19)
