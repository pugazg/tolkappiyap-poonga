# Blossom 55 — Women in War

Those were the times  
Womenfolk were held in regard and concern  
And given due respect for their softness of disposition;

They were therefore kept off places of danger.  
Their duties were restricted to work off the warfront  
And to war camps.

The culture in this regard hasn’t changed;  
What is witnessed today is only a reformulation of things.

***

> The union of love will be kept off  
> From the war-camp, the place of onerous charge.
>
> Women assigned to duties other than love  
> Would be there around in war-camps.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Karpiyal*, verses 34, 35)
