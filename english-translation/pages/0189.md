# Blossom 60 — The Greatness of Women

There is a complaint that ancient works, be it literature or *Tolkāppiyam*, the treasure-trove of grammatical theories, treat women as if they were dumb and servile beings.

Even in this era, which is known for the awakening of women, when war-cries for the rights of women are resounding from every nook and cranny, it could be observed that freedom of women remains a question mark.

We also see that among the intellectuals, there are women just as there are men. What is more, it is a proven fact that women fare better than men in school and college examinations.

Today, one can see them vying with their male counterparts in governance and administration.

In spite of making such strides, here and there women do find themselves a disadvantaged lot thanks to assertions of male superiority.

The number of women who are victims of superstitions, who fall into the pit of orthodoxy, who writhe in ignorance and who are deluded into magic and witchcraft far exceeds the menfolk of such kinds.

Be that as it may, women’s progress is witnessed in fields like science, education and even in the field of defence.
