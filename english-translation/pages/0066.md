# Blossom 15 — The Dream of a Eunuch · continued

“Kaṇṇa! I have a doubt!”

“What is it Arjuṉā? Do come out with it!”

“Am I a male or a female now?”

“Why do you have such a grammatical confusion, all of a sudden?”

“Would I be called he or she?”

“Hold on! Let me refer to *Tolkāppiyam*!”

Thus saying Kaṇṇaṉ stretched his hands towards the opposite direction and from nowhere *Tolkāppiyam*’s palm leaf manuscript came into his hands.

Arjuṉaṉ, who was in the guise of a eunuch posed a question to Kaṇṇaṉ:

“What is this Kaṇṇa? How come *Tolkāppiyam* exists in this age?”

“O the holder of the bow! In the make-believe world of dreams, everything is possible.”

So saying, Kaṇṇaṉ referred to *Tolkāppiyam*.

“Look! *Tolkāppiyam* has an answer to your question!”

“What is the answer? Am I a male or a female?”

“None of the two. Eunuch is the answer.”

“But then, I am a man.”

“Arjuṉā, *Tolkāppiyam* prescribes that a eunuch deprived of manliness should not be represented by the musculine gender. That is, in expressions like ‘eunuch came, went, spoke, etc., only feminine gender should be used, using the proform ‘she’.”

“What is the verse on that?”

“Here! Listen to me as I read!

> Words denoting  
> Hermaphroditic ones of effete musculinity  
> Will not be words with musculine gender endings.”

With disappointment writ large on his face, Arjuṉaṉ beseeched: “Kaṇṇa! Everything is okay. As per *Tolkāppiyam*, anybody sans manliness should be referred to by feminine gender. But then, in my case, I haven’t lost my manliness to become a bisexual like milk spoilt. I am under the guise of a eunuch only to fulfil the condition imposed by gambling.”
