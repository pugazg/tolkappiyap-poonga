# Blossom 45 — Shyness or Honour? · continued

“You are weighed down in anguish, confidante!  
My sweet kindly man he is.  
He maintained his distance with me, like an ascetic.  
Though we met each other daily there was no overstepping on his part.  
While displaying decorum during this secret love career  
One or two streaks of eccentricity did indeed sneak in;  
But we never let that to lead us to fleshly affair.”

“Then how come dear, your cheeks bear injury?  
Bruises on the hand?  
Swelling of fingers?”

Responded the heroine:  
“Both of us met in the rendezvous as usual,  
Which was soon followed by parting.  
Since parents’ approval had been obtained for marriage  
Thanks to the help of a trusted friend like you,  
With the happiness that  
Marriage would follow soon,  
I was crossing the sandy upland, and treaded the way without realizing  
That the shortcut filled with *mullai* flowers was also infested with problems.

A person with a grin approached me and said,  
‘Have you finished the carnal affair one?  
Shall we get on with the next one?’

Since he sought to tarnish the very character of clandestine love career  
I had no desire to even look at that wretched man.  
Therefore, I added spring to steps and yearned to cross that garden  
Before he stretched his hands out to embrace me.

‘Don’t you worry about anything,  
Nobody would find out;  
Let go of shyness, woman,’  
So saying he hemmed me in.

Wriggling away from his probing embrace, I said,  
‘Shyness is superior to very life  
You, a lowly creature worse than a dog!  
Be it known to you!’
