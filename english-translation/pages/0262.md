# Blossom 94 — It Was in Vogue · continued

The judge observes, “It doesn’t seem that Tolkāppiyar included your name wantonly to denigrate your reputation, since Tolkāppiyar lived in an era much before that of yours. How could he contemplate maligning your name as you live in a later period?

Appar replies,

“I accept your explanation regarding difference in the era. Therefore, I do not indict Tolkāppiyar personally. It would be enough if my name be deleted.”

The judge passes the judgement ordering that the name of Appar be deleted.

Therefore, the term *appar* grouped with *mōttai, takar, uṭal* ceased to be there in the list.

Thus was the word *appar* that meant a he-goat made to disappear from the list.

The account above is only an imaginary, humorous anecdote.

Pērāciriyar, the commentator, points out that the convention of using the word *appar* for a he-goat fell out of use in later years.
