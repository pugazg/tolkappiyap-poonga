# Blossom 58 — Monologic Utterances

The ailment called the passion of love  
Sprouts in the morning  
Develops through the day  
And blossoms in the evening.

Is this a disease peculiar to the lady?  
Or does it afflict the man too  
Who wanders like a bee in search of a flower’s honey?

Let there be no argument  
As to whether it is the man or the woman affected.  
Whosoever it might be  
Let’s see what their pleadings with their heart are like:

> The monologic utterances  
> That issue from the mindscapes  
> Of the hero and heroine are:
>
> Addresses to the heart  
> As if it were a living body with sense organs endowed,  
> That manifest the eightfold emotions
