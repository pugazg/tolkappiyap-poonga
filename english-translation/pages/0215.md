# Blossom 72 — Four Sources of Laughter

## Ridicule

( The term *eḷḷal* (ridicule), one of the sources of laughter, has been in vogue for a very long time) Commentators bring in the example of a lame person while talking about the situations where ridicule is due.

( Humane considerations prohibit ridiculing others especially the handicapped. The fast spreading social awareness in the modern world rules out ridicule of a handicapped person’s staggering being a source of laughter.)

However, I would like to share with you an incident I have heard of as an example of the source of laughter.

It was an incident that occurred in a city. Even as civilization has seen great advancement with the advent of industrialization and electrification, people were travelling in bullock carts and carriages driven by horses. There was a vegetable vendor who did business on the roadside. People regularly bought vegetables from him. Whenever a cart passed by, the vendor would become enraged and challenge the occupant with swear words. He would shout, “Hey, horse carriage, bullock cart, go slowly. Why on earth do you raise dust while whizzing past the road? If I get up and come there, you might have to meet with dire consequences. I would whip you severely.”

Though none of the vehicle owners cared to listen to him, the vendor never stopped hurling abuses.
