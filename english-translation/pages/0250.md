# Blossom 89 — Here is the Fifth Source

> Five characteristics the sources of similitude come to  
> As one includes things lowly.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Uvamaiyiyal*, verse 5)

Apart from the four sources of simile -- excellence, beauty, love, might -- mentioned earlier, there is a fifth one too.

That is the comparison of the subject with the lowly things.

The Tamil term *kiḻakkiṭu poruḷ* used in the Tolkāppiyam verse is a trivial thing or something degrading or lowly.

In a big village, a person was working as a washerman assisted by five donkeys. On an eventful night, one of those donkeys was found missing. He went searching for it throughout the night. At the approach of dawn, the washerman thought he heard a donkey bray from a house. “Hurray! I have traced my donkey”, he exulted and entered that house. Actually, it was the singer in the house doing his early morning practice.

The singer was surprised to see this man sprint in, and enquired, “What is the matter?”

He replied, “I have lost my donkey and I am searching for it. Since your music sounded like my donkey’s voice I rushed in here.”

“Now get lost! Stop barking like a dog and get out!” he shouted in rage.
