# Blossom 44 — The Wet Firewood that Burned · continued

And commenting that the rendezvous was fine;  
The towering trees, plants and bushes  
Dropped their flowers in the wind  
And did a good turn,  
For they made for a fine floral bed!

Nobody was around,  
Only a bit of fear remained as companion for him.

Guarded by culture native to womenfolk,  
That naive full moon of a damsel was feasting upon  
The external beauty of that man.

He expected her to acquiesce in his desire for sex,  
As does a camphor,  
Without patiently waiting for the wet wood to dry out and ready to burn.

Though she gave her consent to the union of hearts  
She didn’t acquiesce in bodily contact  
And sit aside she did.

She then gave him an oblique look which drank his life  
He understood the underlying consent.  
He thought, “Why not take the fruit and relish it?  
I am granted the consent  
Not just for two souls to become one  
But also for two bodies to unite.”  
He tended to cross the limits.

When she tried to stop him,  
He said, “I beseech you!  
We both are meeting amidst many a hurdle  
Do realize, dear.”

His hands moved about caressing her.  
She gestured as if saying,  
“Leave alone the hurdles, now take away the finger probing my waist.”  
She moved a little away from him.
