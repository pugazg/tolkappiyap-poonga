# Blossom 71 — Manifest Emotions Explained Further

*Meyppāṭu* is an inner feeling manifesting on one’s body for others to see and hear. Watery eyes, beads of tears, bristling of hair, perspiration, shivering and other such overt signs are the *meyppāṭus* arising out of the mind’s response to an external event.

Suppose a person faces a python coming to swallow him, it is natural for him to be subjected to *shivering of the body*, anxiety to escape and perspiration. The python here is the external event (*cuvaippaṭuporuḷ*) that causes the manifestation of the emotions (*cuvai*) on the person, whose tripidation and bewilderment are the signs (*kuṟippu*) and perspiration and shivering are the physical expressions (*viṟal*). These things together, as they come to be known to others are described by the term *meyppāṭu*.

It has been the part of *literary conventions* from the ancient times to represent these sentiments arising out of our inner life and external life situations and experiences in dramatic usage and imaginative usage.
