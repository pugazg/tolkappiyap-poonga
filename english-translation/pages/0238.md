# Blossom 83 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Fourth Stage of Physical Manifestations

The sweet clandestine union having enjoyed,  
The lovers part  
With hearts full of love.

Another day --

The kindred folks spot them.  
With an open-hearted praise of the lady,  
The hero said:  
“I have resolved to marry her”.  
But the people of the village are not amused.
