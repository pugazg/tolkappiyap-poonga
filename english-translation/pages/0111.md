“I am proud of parenting a brave son. My arms swell up in excitement, to see you stand seeking permission, with a sword in your hand. My heart quivers in excitement. Yes, proceed towards the fort. Annihilate all the wings of foe’s army, be it the front rank, cavalry, infantry or the elephant wing. You have obtained the permission, dear prince. March on!”

After getting the king’s permission, the elite wing of the army raised the war cry “Victory is ours” and marched forward to the beating of the drums.

The doors of the fort got shattered! The flag pole fell down! With nobody left to save the country, the nation of veṭci men became a virtual hunted ground for the karaṇtai warriors. “We are unparalleled” was the song on the lips of the karaṇtai soldiers.

The prince raised the sword, which fetched victory to him and his army and entered the fort riding his horse. At the time of entry, a spear flying from nowhere pierced through his chest.

He held the fatal spear and breathed his last, but not before accounting for ten to twenty heads of the enemy soldiers.

“Dear son! You declined the golden crown and rather chose the aura of fame through bravery,” a visibly perturbed king kissed his forehead. The soldiers, unable to celebrate their victory fully, erected a hero-stone for the immortal prince in tune with the practice of raising hero-stones in memory of the warriors dying undaunted in the field of battle.
