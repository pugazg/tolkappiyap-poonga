# Blossom 30 — The Heroine of the Agricultural Tract · continued

“I am the tank most suitable for a lotus like you to float on.”

He grabs her and pulls her towards him. But in a defiant fury, she pushes him aside and makes good her escape. Dushyandhan of the drama stands stupefied relishing even her anger.

This one-sided love is what is called *kaikkiḷai*.

***

The defining features of unrequited love conduct (*kaikkiḷai*) are:

> One falls in for a lass  
> With the passion of sex unformed yet  
> And yet,  
> One turns incurably afflicted;  
> One enters into conversation with that lass  
> Which alternates between praise and dispraise;  
> No reciprocity on her part marked though,  
> One persists indulgently in one’s address.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse 50).
