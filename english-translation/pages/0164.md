# Blossom 49 — Clandestine Love Career and Wedded Course · continued

And make a blissful living to the acclaim of the world.

The order of Tolkāppiyar’s day was that  
The hero stood on a higher plane  
In the domestic sphere and in other realms of activity too.

Being so,  
In this flower-garden of *Tolkāppiyam* that I write,  
I make no attempt whatever to change that order.

Tolkāppiyar steers clear of any controversy  
With regard to God or religion he believed in  
And holds forth God and religion universal.

Such is the way Aiyaṉ Tiruvaḷḷuvar has chosen later,  
Keeping religion and God secular.

The *Akam* lovers are seen running their household  
In tune with the norms of both Tolkāppiyar and Tiruvaḷḷuvar.

The bud called clandestine love course  
Blossoms into the flower called wedded course  
By marriage.

*Malivu* the ecstasy of union of the lovers,  
*Pulavi* the feigned reserve of the heroine,  
*Ūṭal* the sulking of the lovers leading to sexual fulfilment,  
*Uṇarvu* the resolution of sulking through the interceders,  
And *pirivu* the separation occasioned by the parting of the hero  
In quest of wealth and learning  
And for acts of valour for the country’s good --  
Occur during the wedded course after marriage.

“O my manna pure! Honey unsatiating!  
Do tell me without concealing.  
During those days of our clandestine career,  
When we were showering love unexhausted on each other,  
Why did you try to draw my heart apart  
And drown it in the flood of devotion to God?”
