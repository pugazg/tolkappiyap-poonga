# Blossom 28 — The Call of Duty and the Predicament of the Hero

Along with the gooseberry tree soaked in the nectar of blooming *mullai* creeper and with the same fruit from the tree being fed upon by the doe, the *mullai* creeper hugging the tree making it look like a woman around her man, stirs the hero’s love memories of the moments of union with his beloved. He finds himself in a dilemma over being parted from his love, even as he prepares himself to depart on a mission seeking knowledge.

But is it wisdom to get overwhelmed by love and fail to understand the nature of separation from the beloved? Doesn’t one have his responsibilities for earning? Further, he has to sharpen his knowledge and intelligence. He should lead a life of dignity and should not come to be treated with disdain as an ignorant person. He should discharge his duty as a member of the society by empowering himself with knowledge. Doesn’t he have the responsibility to acquire wealth through rightful means, and also set an example for others by his moral stature? Without getting emotionally affected by the separation, he should undertake the task at hand, guided by propriety and fortitude.

The realities of the possible hurdles he might face on his mission, however, come to mind. Is the foe’s strength to be underestimated? One should properly assess the enemy’s strength, his army’s strength, and ally’s strength, while simultaneously weighing in foot-soldiers,cavalry, and elephant force also does not go unnoticed.
