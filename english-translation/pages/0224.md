# Blossom 75 — Four Sources of Wonder · continued

## Minuteness (ciṟumai)

Scholars hail *Tirukkuṟaḷ* as having drilled a hole in an atom and stuffed into it knowledge equivalent to seven seas.

Yes, an atom is that small. But bombs made of such atoms not only brought curtains upon the Second World War but also extinguished the flame of life in millions of people. The immense potential of the tiny atoms stupefies everybody, doesn’t it?

## Transmutation (ākkam)

The ancient people of the country remained helpless and were subjected to humiliation from all quarters. Ambedkar the great emerged as the ring of protection for them, crossing swords with the all-powerful ‘Varnashra Dharma’ and coming out victorious.

The spectacle of K. R. Narayanan occupying the chair of the President of India is an example of the evolutionary development of the principles of Ambedkar, the architect of the Constitution of India. Such a social transmutation achieved by Ambedkar is but a matter of wonder.

The following is the *Tolkāppiyam* verse in this regard:

> The four sources of wonder that overwhelm one’s mind are  
> Novelty, hugeness, minuteness and transmutation.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Meyppāṭṭiyal*, verse 7)

**Note:** Men of wisdom are capable of comprehending the true nature of newness, hugeness, minuteness and transmutation. As such, the emotion of wonder is that of others who are witness to these four phenomena. Perhaps for this reason, Tolkāppiyar in the verse above speaks of these phenomena as overwhelming ones.
