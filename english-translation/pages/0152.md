# Blossom 45 — Shyness or Honour? · continued

Young lady, don’t sport shyness;  
Give me your virginity for me to enjoy  
Giving up your bashfulness,’ he said.

‘You, the wolf of a man! Do understand one thing,  
Shyness is superior to life and chastity is superior to shyness.  
I declare right now,  
I am prepared to shed my shyness.  
Looking at shyness as a weakness in women  
You dare to defile me crossing me on the street,  
Don’t you?

Here, I shed shyness to save my chastity on my own.  
I swirled like a whirlwind and hurled a big stone at him.  
He also didn’t remain silent,  
And transformed himself into a sword to subjugate me.  
If I had donned the shyness,  
Pondering, ‘Should one assault a man?’  
I would have lost my honour and chastity.

Now do you understand the reason for my injury, friend?  
Shyness is superior to life  
While honour is superior to shyness itself.  
That honour is woman’s chastity.” she concluded.

The confidante stood up glad at my deed  
And she took leave of me then.

***

> As the heroine goes herself  
> Seeking the hero she has fallen in love with,  
> Or as she says such words as are freed of wilfulness and guile,  
> Armed she is  
> With the saying of the ancients that  
> ‘Bashfulness is of greater worth than life itself,  
> And chastity unsullied stands superior to bashfulness too’ --  
> And things lofty such as these  
> Constitute the thematic modes of *Akam* love proper.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Kaḷaviyal*, verse 22)
