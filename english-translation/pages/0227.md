# Blossom 77 — Four Sources of Pride

## Learning (kalvi)

There is education where one learns about the world in a book and also the other kind where the world itself is the book to be read. Anybody excelling in both these types of education will naturally stand at the peak of knowledge. Such people had excelled in the past and do so at the present time as well. When the entire world showers praise upon them, we, as do these people, are filled with a sense of pride.

## Prowess (tarukaṇ)

Those with an unflinching resolve with the flame of truth burning within can stand up to any challenge without fear. They are very brave indeed. Such people find a place among men of *prowess*.

## Fame (icaimai)

With the golden saying, “If at all you live in this world, do so with fame” as their motto, people from all walks of life yearn for recognition and fame. Fame follows only those who *don’t covet it*.
