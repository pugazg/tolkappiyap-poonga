# Blossom 44 — The Wet Firewood that Burned · continued

“Yes, yes!”

At that time she was standing unseen behind the surrounding wall.  
The hero pretending to be unaware of her presence  
Described her loveliness from head to toe:  
“She is the pinnacle of beauty;  
When she dresses her hair in five modes --  
Bundling it up,  
Letting it flowing,  
Or decorating it in the curled fashion--  
My darting eyes  
Would ask who this enchanting woman is!  
Is she the angel from heaven?  
Is she the one called Ramba or Urvaci?  
Dear friend, why don’t you say something to please my craving heart?  
Would your life-span decrease if you do so?”

So saying  
The excited hero stamped on the plank of the chariot  
That spurred the scared horses to run along with the chariot  
And they were about to ram against the lightning-like heroine!

He pulled her away and exclaimed  
“Oh! Is that you?  
I didn’t know the richly-hued peahen would be here around!  
O my goodness, you are spared!”  
“Dear, hope you are not hurt,” he asked with concern.

Taking not his hands held around her, he said,  
“I have been yearning for it for a long time.”  
And bashfulness prevented her from responding,  
“I too.”

“The Cupid has struck his arrow, now I don’t have any business here.”  
The dear friend said  
And he left the place within the bating of the eyelid, in the chariot,  
But not before winking at the hero
