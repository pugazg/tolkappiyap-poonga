# Blossom 85 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Sixth Stage of Physical Manifestations

The lover could draw her unto him  
Like the flavour of sugar cane, juice of the fruit and a piece of magnet.  
But how could she forget the memories of her lying on her mother’s lap  
Growing under the protective care of her parents?

That is why --

The imminent parting from parents --  
She wears an anguished countenance  
As she is decked in bridal wear.  
Though a sea of kinsmen swarm around her,  
She feels like being on an island.
