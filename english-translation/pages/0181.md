# Blossom 56 — The Horse and the Bird would Stand Outsmarted!

Mission abroad accomplished,

Vēḷaṉ was returning home on his fleeting horse  
Eager as he was to see his beloved wife.

The tears shed by the sky would bring happiness to the peasants,  
While Vēḷaṉ’s return alone could infuse life into his woman.

The flower doesn’t wither  
If the colourful beetle fails to visit to relish its honey,  
While the young woman’s heart might turn into a turbulent earthquake  
If the horse carrying Vēḷaṉ failed to be back home.  
She might breathe like a storm and writhe like a worm.
