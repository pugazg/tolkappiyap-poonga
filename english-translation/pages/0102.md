# Blossom 30 — The Heroine of the Agricultural Tract · continued

Lo and behold! King Dushyandhan and the sage’s daughter Cakuntalai meet by a wild stream in the forest.

“Cakuntala, this memorable meeting is the foundation of our immortal love.”

“You are the king, here in the forest for hunting.

You took me for a deer and hunted me. How could an ascetic’s hut bank upon palace’s word of treating this meeting as love’s base?”

“I have encountered a number of animals while hunting. But I feel that you and the love induced by you have become a fetter to me. Now I won’t move even a foot towards the palace.”

“No. Don’t relinquish your duties towards the country. And don’t wash your hands off this forest flower either.”

“You are certainly not an emerald flower; instead, you are the flower of my life. Yes, you are the essence of my life!”

“Hope you won't forget me!”

“Forget? How can I forget this embodiment of beauty? Here, have my signet ring as a symbol of my love unforgettable.”

Dushyandhan puts his signet ring on Cakuntalai’s finger and kisses.

(During the break, they both come to the dressing room for change of make-up).

The artiste playing Dushyandhan approaches the actress taking Cakuntalai’s role and speaks to her, “What Tāmarai! I planted a sizzling kiss on your fingers, while putting on the ring. How did it taste?” he bared his teeth and smiled.

“In the drama only, I was Cakuntalai! You were Dushyandhan!”

“Why can’t we remain so in real life?”

“Kindly watch your manners. What is your age? And what is mine?”

“I am a well-built young man and you are a bud today! But the bud would soon become a flower, dear girl, provided you fall for a man like me.”

“Don’t nurse unrealistic dreams. Go away.”

“The next scene in the play would be my forgetting you.”

“That scene appears, only after the signet ring is lost.”

“Before the ring gets lost, what about embracing you and planting a kiss?”

“Let that ring be lost right now.”

So saying, she throws away the signet ring in the dressing room itself. She walks off in a huff and disrobes her ascetic guise, even while going outside.”

“Oh girl! The signet ring should be swallowed by the fish. Isn’t that the real story of the play?”

“Before that, you might gobble me!”
