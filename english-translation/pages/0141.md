# Blossom 42 — Speaking through the Eyes · continued

Of no avail whatever are words of mouth  
Where eyes meet eyes in acquiescence true.

The love which was like a flower has now grown into a green fruit.  
They knew that in keeping with the Tamil ethos,  
They should wait till their love turned into a ripe fruit.  
That is the way of the Tamils prescribed in *Tolkāppiyam*.

“The union of two hearts is not a short story.  
I convey my greetings for it to become a never-ending saga;  
Depart for the day and come tomorrow.”  
So said the genial friend.

Since it was time for the devotees to throng the temple,  
They decided that the day’s converse of the eyes was enough and parted.

The confidant clutched the shoulder of the hero and said,  
“The woman is a beauty endowed with timidity, bashfulness and credulity.”

The hero intervened and corrected the friend’s note:  
“The dame fell into fear as I touched her;  
Bashfulness is an inborn virtue unto women,  
And I don’t accept ignorance in women.  
If both of us were given to ignorance  
Wouldn’t it lead to physical relationship?

We the birds of love  
Have a perfect understanding that  
The clandestine career is one  
Where the hearts unite  
With no room for fleshly congress,  
And so we parted after our hearts united into one.”

After the hero finished his address

By signs healthy
