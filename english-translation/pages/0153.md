# Blossom 46 — Two Interpretations : An Explanation

Kōvalan the son of Mācāttuvān  
And Kaṇṇaki the daughter of Māṇāykaṉ  
Were united in wedlock  
On the auspicious hour of the auspicious day,  
The vedic rites being prescribed by the ripe brahmin priest  
And the bridal couple circumambulating the sacrificial fire.

The auspicious time and day prescribed  
Did not guard against the separation of Kōvalan  
From the virtuous Kaṇṇaki,  
The woman on whom Kōvalan on the nuptial bed showered praises like  
‘Gold with no blemish’  
‘Pearl with right-turned curls’  
‘Fragrance unadulterated’  
‘Sugar cane’ and ‘honey’;  
Nor did they protect the nuptial cord of Kaṇṇaki.  
They but pierced through his wedded life like swords and spears.
