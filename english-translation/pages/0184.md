# Blossom 57 — What is a Fortune? · continued

And having reaped all that it offered,  
Will have their fortune realized  
As they live surrounded by pleasant children  
And comfort-giving kindred folks  
And live in serenity  
Taking to ways of virtue.

***

> Having relished the felicities of the passion of love,  
> It is for the hero and heroine in conjugal union  
> To savour the protective presence of their children  
> And seek to live on things sublime,  
> With virtuous kinsfolk around,  
> Which makes for the fruits the wedded course entailed.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Karpiyal*, verse 51)
