# Blossom 50 — The Light of Home · continued

When the martyrs who sacrificed for the freedom of the land  
Were remembered with grateful admiration.  
The lady was floating on the ocean of joy.

Then she heard the tidings of her man  
Drawn in passion to an alien woman.

It was a blow as if by an axe;  
She writhed in pain like a worm thrown into fire.

He was worried about  
Her turning a tiger and a storm.  
The hill-like man stood reduced in his standing.

He approached her companions  
With a request to quench  
The raging fire in the heart of the heroine.  
The efforts of them came to no avail.

Eventually,  
He went to her in the bedchamber  
Held her legs in tight hold  
And begged her pardon.

Could she say ‘no’ to him?  
Or could she erase his image from her mind?  
She did pardon him.

“May the one who coined the word ‘pardon’ live long!”  
Thinking thus  
The hero moved closer to her  
And she moved off a little  
Saying, “There is the child!”

***

> Bearing him a son worthy of his name;
>
> As gifts aplenty are bestowed on the sages  
> And worthy offerings on the celestials  
> At the hour of the heroine
