# Blossom 13 — Commercial Competency · continued

“We have Gandhi’s *My Experiments with Truth*.”

Kaṇṇaki’s father responded, “I have that. Therefore, I don’t need it! It is okay. What about Aṇṇā’s *Iru Paramparaikaḷ*?”

Gokhale went back to stroking his hair.

“The latest edition has arrived. Aṇṇā’s *Veḷḷai Māḷikaiyil*. Would you need it? We have that book.”

“So you don’t possess *Iru Paramparaikaḷ*? Doesn’t matter’. *Veḷḷai Māḷikaiyil* referred to by you is available in our own Kaṇṇaki library.”

“Well, so you own a library? Do you have at least a thousand books?” Aruṇaṉ posed the question to Kaṇṇaki and ogled her. She couldn’t bear it. She turned the other way round and searched for books!

At that juncture, Aruṇaṉ’s father determined to clinch the business deal, briefed Kaṇṇaki’s parents, “*Tirukkuṟaḷ* commentaries are available!”

“Kindly get us Pulavar Kuḻantai’s *Rāvaṇa Kāviyam*, if at all you possess it!”

“Well, *Rāmāyaṇam* is available, which is widely read by everybody!”

Kaṇṇaki intervened, “Not that. Do you have Pāratitācaṉ’s *Pāṇṭiyaṉ Paricu*?”

As if waiting for this opportune moment, Aruṇaṉ replied wearing a flustered face, “We have *Etirpārāta Muttam* (Unexpected Kiss). Do you need it?”. He looked at Kaṇṇaki and winked.

Kaṇṇaki retorted sternly, “I have *Tamiḻacciyiṉ Katti* (Tamil Woman’s Knife)” and after a pause, she said, “Even that is authored by Pāratitācaṉ.” Her eyes spit fire. Aruṇaṉ was subdued.

Her irritated father questioned the shop owner, “Well, you have been mentioning about some other books instead of the ones asked for! What joke is this?” Proprietor Gokhale smiled and clarified it, “Sir, this is the advice of *Tolkāppiyam*.

A businessman should follow only that. One should never say ‘No’ to a customer; instead, whatever is available in the shop should be made known. Then only would business prosper. This is what he says.”

Kaṇṇaki’s mother who remained tightlipped so far, enquired, “Well then! Do you at least have *Tolkāppiyam* commentary?”

Pat came the reply from Proprietor Gokhale, “The cheap edition of *Mahābāratam* is available.”
