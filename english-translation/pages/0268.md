# Blossom 97 — Those Who Realize will Know, Those Who Know will Realize · continued

This verse emphasizes that words not breaching the established conventions must go into the making of a verse.

I am not adept in the grammar of poetry. I brought out, sixty years ago, a book of verses titled “It is No Poetry”, wherein I have openly admitted my verses being metreless.

I have presided at many poetic meets of eminent poets through years. And this is what I wrote as my presidential address in the poetic meet, on the theme ‘Freedom Fighters’ broadcast on the All India Radio, Tiruchi, in the year 1967.

> He in prison lived, and pulled the oilpress,  
> To drive away the Whites who ruled the land.  
> Many were killed before the prime of their lives.  
> He fought like a sword drawn from out of the sheath,  
> With no care for his life.  
> Of his glory to hear,  
> We are all arrayed here today.
>
> But to describe the greatness of all those  
> Who have their lives laid down for freedom,  
> I have neither the poetic ability acquired,  
> Nor the art of poetry learnt.
>
> Though my verses might poetic metre lack,  
> Guided as I was by the sole principle  
> That our land, language, honour and feelings  
> Should not go undefended,  
> I make poetic lines of my feelings,  
> That from my heart do gush forth,  
> To let those treacherous ones,  
> The turncoats of principles, gain awareness  
> I shall say this.
>
> Freedom was gained,  
> By the loss of so many lives, by the loss of so much blood!  
> After great loss and labour on earth,  
> Freedom, like purest gold, was earned.
