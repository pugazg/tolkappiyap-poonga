# Blossom 39 — Instability of Life and Things Worldly · continued

> *Kāñci* is  
> Where the woman  
> Clasps her husband’s spear-pierced body  
> As does a creeper over the banyan trunk.
>
> *Kāñci* is  
> Where the wife leaps lightning-like into the flames  
> Detesting life beyond her husband  
> When he remained dead - apart  
> A sacrifice it is of womankind eye-like  
> To show that life is no sugar cane  
> And to hold honour dear as life.  
> Such is what *kāñci* is.
>
> No more energy I am left with  
> To elaborate further on this *kāñci* theme;  
> I stop it therefore at this.
