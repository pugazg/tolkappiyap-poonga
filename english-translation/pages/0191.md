# Blossom 61 — Without the Fox Getting Drenched...!

The smithy forging implements for war it was  
Where soldiers from the army camp were at work.  
There came a young maiden running, holding a letter of palmyra leaf.

A warrior-hero engaged in the work exclaimed:  
“You are the woman who can bathe the fox without getting it wet!  
I presumed you would come tomorrow.  
But today just as I am making this sword,  
You have arrived to gladden my heart.  
What letter did my flower-eyed woman give?  
Give it!” So saying he received the letter  
Without minding the taunts and comments from those around.

The text of the letter ran:  
I expected your visit during daytime yesterday and waited with hunger;  
Then only I realized that all your assurances are like letters written on thin air.  
I even became apprehensive of a friend’s prediction that our love would fail.
