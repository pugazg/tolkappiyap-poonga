> *Veṭci* is  
> Men at the border-posts,  
> Charged by their ruler,  
> Capturing the cows of the hostile king by stealth  
> And keeping them right under guard.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Puṟattiṇai iyal*, verse 2)

Coming to *veṭci* situation, there are fourteen types of activities (called sub-situations (*tuṟais*)) attendant on it. These activities in their order of occurrence are enumerated in *Tolkāppiyam* as under:

> The fourteen substrands of the *veṭci* theme are:  
> The clamour  
> That marks the army’s movement to lift the foeman’s cows;  
> The utterances of townsfolk around  
> Coming for good omens for the marching men;  
> The men’s march  
> Being unknown to the foeman’s spies  
> That infiltrate into their midst;  
> The marching men’s spying  
> Unnoticed by the foeman;  
> Lying in wait for the moment opportune to lift the cows  
> Encircling the foemen’s positions spied right;  
> Slaying of foemen that offer resistance;  
> Capturing the cows therein;  
> Putting to rout the foemen in pursuit;  
> Being back home
