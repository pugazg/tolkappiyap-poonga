# Blossom 49 — Clandestine Love Career and Wedded Course · continued

But the dreams of their furtive career refused to die down!  
They went about recalling in joy those days  
When many a hurdle ran into their way  
Which they overcame using many a strategy  
And when their companions came to their aid unfailing.  
And they glossed over things unsavoury too  
When there intervened the beat of the drum called sulking.

“One day I stood waiting there for you  
Till my legs threatened to break  
And you did not turn up!”

The hero said in response quick:  
“None too good were you too!  
Another day  
I waited for you sleepless through the night  
And you failed to turn up!”

All these accusations mutual  
Amounted at length to letters written on the air!

The interceders stepping in,  
The false sullen mood  
Gave way to the bliss of joy ensuing.  
Should we need to tell those versed in this art  
That sulking adds to the joy of union?
