# Blossom 59 — What did the Heart Say?

The heart comes to realize that  
The corporal frame is gradually getting emaciated and life exhausted.  
Many days have passed since she met her husband on the bed.

Therefore, she realizes that only a little amount of life is left in her.  
With the heart being kept unaware,  
She questions herself in anguish:

Whatever has happened to my body?  
Whatever is the affliction to my life?  
Leaving the questions at that,  
She makes a resolution:  
‘See him I shall not!’

> Even when the lonesome heroine turns dispirited  
> And wears withering, emaciated limbs,  
> She never goes out seeking the hero’s union,  
> But abides, exclaiming  
> ‘O whatever has befallen them!’

(*Poruḷatikāram : Poruḷiyal*, verse 9)
