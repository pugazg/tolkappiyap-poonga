# Blossom 24 — A Simple Point in the Middle · continued

Even as union, separation, etc., may occur in all classes of land, we need to look at *tiṇai* as a place or land of occurrence of an event of love.

*Puṟattiṇai* is the parallel, the counterpoint of *Akattiṇai*. While *Akam* is the intimacy of love behaviour and conjugal life between a man and a woman, *Puṟam* signifies aspects of life in the society at large such as war, governance, learning, friendship, etc. The *Puṟam* events, like the *Akam* ones, are both land-specific and universal.

This simple explanation would be useful in understanding the issues of *akam* and *puṟam* life in their complexities - the breeze, the storm, the tremors of the earth in the life of men and women.
