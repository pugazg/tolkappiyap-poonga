# Blossom 82 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Third Stage of Physical Manifestations · continued

And tucks it up  
Running her palm down to the hip.  
Just then  
A gush of strong wind snatches away her upper garment.  
Crossing her elbows over the chest  
And placing the fingers ten on the shoulders,  
She conceals her breasts  
As does one  
That hides the stolen lotus buds  
From the guards of the pond.  
These four  
Along with displaying non-existent strength,  
Constitute the bodily manifestations  
Of the third phase of love union.  
They then leave the rendezvous  
With two bodies but with hearts unseparated.

* * *

> The third phase of the clandestine love manifests in:  
> Her seeing her hips down off from exposure as the garments slip,  
> Correcting the waist-ornaments thereon,  
> Her pretence to be unassailed by slings of passion,  
> And her hands rising, her pretence notwithstanding.
