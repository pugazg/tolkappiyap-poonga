# Blossom 80 — Fivefold Akam Love: The First Stage of Physical Manifestations

While looking at the physical manifestations proper to the fivefold *Akam* love, it seems that the *Meyppāṭṭiyal* section of *Tolkāppiyam* may have inspired and aided the great bard Kampar. While describing the scene of the first meeting between Cītā who was standing on the terrace of the palace at Mitilai and Rāma who was ambling along the streets accompanied by the sage Viswamitrā, Kampar states:

The Lord looked at her, which she also reciprocated.

I must add a further gleam to Kampar’s imagination:

**The Lord looked at her**  
**Which she also reciprocated,**
