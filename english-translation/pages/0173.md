# Blossom 51 — Kumaraṉ’s Message · continued

Would you be gifted with a child?

My parents used to say that I was born ten years after their marriage.  
If my parents had also remained parted in sulks for having been issueless,  
How could I have been born?

Now also, there are quarrels and sulks marking our conjugal life.

I ventured to a foreign country in pursuit of wealth.  
‘My life would be shrouded in darkness, therefore I would also come.’  
She insisted.  
How can I change the convention against women taking up voyage?

With her eyes flooded with tears,  
She quarrelled and remained silent for a few days.  
I could not change the age-old custom of ancestors,  
But I changed my plan of going on a voyage,  
I travelled to the nearby capital city for training in warfare for ten days.

‘You assured me that there wouldn’t be any separation.  
I didn’t know then that you were lying.’  
She fired a letter on the fifth day itself.

Then, what work could I have there?  
I beat a hasty retreat midway,  
To wipe off her misery caused by parting.

Compromise has never been a one way path in my house.

I had committed a big blunder when she gave birth to my son.  
A fury she did become!  
But on my apology she forgot what had happened.

Unlike in my case,  
No male chauvinistic blunder has happened here.  
Therefore you should forget the quarrel with your man.  
You should extend a whole-hearted cooperation.  
May you be blessed with the fruit of your thousand year crop, your married  
life!”

Such of these arguments and many more I presented before her.  
“Nothing equals the bliss of married life.  
The life of the householder is a virtue unto itself.
