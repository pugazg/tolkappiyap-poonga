# Blossom 46 — Two Interpretations : An Explanation · continued

As meaning sport and festivities.  
He says  
The hero takes part in the sports of the town  
In the company of his close companions,  
That would act as a cover  
Protecting him from the scandal of the villagefolk  
About his visit to the heroine’s place.

And Veḷḷaivāraṇar provides us several instances,  
Not just one or two,  
Where *ōrai* refers to play and festivities.

Going by which  
We can establish that  
Tolkāppiyar uses the term *ōrai*  
In the sense of play  
And not auspicious hour.

Where we insist that  
Veḷḷaivāraṇar is unacceptable  
And that *ōrai* is observing propitious hour,  
It might then mean that  
Beliefs in day and time auspicious  
Are an intrusion into the Tamiḻ life  
As is the visitation of snakes and scorpions.

> For his rendezvous with the heroine.
>

> From his rendezvous with the heroine

> And by the fear of perils and obstacles to his love.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Kaḷaviyal*, verse 45)
