# Blossom 96 — The Five Elements · continued

Now, what does the expression in Tamil *kiṇaṟu veṭṭap pūtam puṟappaṭṭatu* (the elements surfaced as the well was dug) mean?

As one digs up the well, the ‘earth’ piles up - one element.  
The ‘air’ shut up in the earth comes out - second element  
The ‘water’ gushes forth - third element  
The ‘sky’ is reflected in the water - fourth element  
The sun’s ‘fire’ purifies the water - fifth element.

Thus remains the world as the structured fusion of the elements. The phenomena of the world must therefore be denoted without deviation from the norms of usage, and in terms of the human--non-human generic distinction and the five genders informing them.

Violation of the norms of usage would result in distortion and variation of meanings of words. The things of the world must be represented without deviation from the established conventions and in accord with human--non-human classification and gender distinction. Thus goes the import of the Tolkāppiyam verse.

Pērāciriyar’s explanation is that the five elements remain individual entities and at the same time they remain fused with the things of the world.
