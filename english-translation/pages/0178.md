# Blossom 53 — Tour

Without being confined to the house like a cat that has littered,  
Without living like a caged parrot taking the cage for the nation,  
The hero and heroine would take to going out on tours at least once a year.  
They followed this practice even in those days.

Taking their children along, they played in soothing ponds and beautiful  
parks,  
Enjoying the beauty in nature.

How sweet it is to know that  
The Tamils lived a complete life  
Even in the infancy of their history,  
Which *Tolkāppiyam* shows  
In its section on the wedded life!

***

> Seeking the pleasures of life,  
> Such as sports in rivers and tanks  
> And revelry in groves,  
> Beyond the bounds of their home soil,  
> Is part of the love career of the hero and heroine.
