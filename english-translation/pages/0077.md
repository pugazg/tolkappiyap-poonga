# Blossom 20 — Leadership Traits

Having discussed the features of land and time and the modes of love behaviour, it is now necessary to explain the distinctive regional features of the five tracts of land (*karupporuḷ*).

The deity worshipped, food for the stomach, animals, trees, plants, creepers, birds, leather instruments, occupation, the lyre and other string instruments and things of these sorts would come under *karupporuḷ*. Place, water, flower, etc., would also come under *karupporuḷ*.

> God, food, animal, tree,  
> Bird, drum, occupation and the lute  
> And such of these  
> Constitute the distinctive physical features of the given tract of land.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verse 18)

Though, in this list of *karupporuḷ*, flowers and birds are allotted to specific divisions of land, they might be shown to belong to other divisions of land.

> Where the flora and fauna occur  
> Not marked proper to the land and season in question though,  
> They be deemed  
> Characteristic of that land and that season.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Akattiṇai iyal*, verses 19)
