# Blossom 97 — Those Who Realize will Know, Those Who Know will Realize · continued

> He wouldn’t bend like a question mark on the bed  
> But would lie down erect like an arrow with legs stretched out.
>
> Is he one with a backbone unbending?  
> Is his heart a field  
> Where the crop of obeisance never grows?  
> Amazed thus were many  
> With gaping mouths and fingers on the nose.

Yet another poem:

> There came a woman  
> Swinging like a jewelled chariot,  
> Walking on the verdant grass  
> Where the eggs of snow were being hatched.
>
> She was an adept in the flow of fruit-like Tamil words;  
> She was the elder sister of Kākkaippāṭiṉi Poṉmuṭiyār;  
> She sang the praises of  
> Atiyamāṉ Neṭumāṉañci and the munificent Pāri,  
> Cēramāṉ Māveṅkōvaṉ,  
> Kānapper tanta Vaḷuti  
> And the Cōḻa king Perunaṟkiḷḷi,  
> Thereby adding many a jewel to Tamil,  
> She is Avvai, the light of wisdom!

Another instance:

> Honour! That’s the lullaby my son listened to;  
> Death! That’s the sport he played;  
> I taught him the art of using lances  
> Even as he was a suckling;
>
> He turned a worthy son to his father  
> As aerial roots to a banyan tree;
>
> He rose against the foes in rage,  
> The foes with stunted minds like the goat’s tail,  
> Who dared to usurp our throne,  
> He flayed them and saw them routed  
> And he gave his life in the deed.
