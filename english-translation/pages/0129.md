# Blossom 38 — The Light amidst the Darkness · continued

> The eight qualities that mark a courtier  
> [Nobility of birth, learning, virtuous conduct,  
> Truthfulness, purity, equity  
> And absence of envy and greed];  
> Such modes of conduct as enjoined by sage works;  
> Unceasing acts of liberality that entail lasting renown;  
> Bearing no malice towards men that do evil unto one;  
> Being endowed with the wealth of the spirit;  
> Mercy and compassion as marks the detached mind;  
> And being freed of the fetters of want.
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Puṟattiṇai iyal*, verse 21)
