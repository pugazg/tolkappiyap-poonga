# Blossom 36 — Persistence in War like an Unyielding Buffalo

The spectacle of the tumpai puram situation of a king taking on the enemy king of undaunted prowess and determination and putting him to rout is one of the finest examples of ancient Tamiḻs’ valour capable of moving even the stony hearts. In the battle arena, where the two kings cross swords, the enemy soldiers, afraid of going near a fighter, aim spears and bows at him from a safe distance. The warrior, carrying a sword and a shield, fights with all his might. His sword chops off many a number of enemies, but gets blunted after brushing against a stone. His shield also develops chinks and he offers his body as a shield to take on the innumerable spears and arrows shot at him. Alas!
