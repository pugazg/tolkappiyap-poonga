# Blossom 24 — A Simple Point in the Middle

It is a common trait among scholars - of the past as well as the present -- who have penned commentaries on the old Tamiḻ words and phrases to overlook the comprehension potential of the readers.

Instead of providing simple explanations, they prefer to tread on rough terrain, by being pedantic like classical musicians, who flaunt all the finer nuances of music in a concert, to be appreciated by only a hundred people among multitudes of laymen.

To be precise, how many persons know as to what exactly *tiṇai* means?

*Tiṇai* is the term related to aspects of behaviour, land division and classes of beings. While *uyartiṇai* refers to human beings, *akṟiṇai* denotes things non-human.

*Akattiṇai* signifies the love career of the hero and the heroine through the phases of union, separation, patient waiting, sulking, etc., and deals with the pre-marital and marital relations of these two, with *akam*, the heart, standing at the base. There is also the unrequited love and abnormal sexual passion.

*Aintiṇai* is the fivefold love career of the hero and the heroine -- *kuṟiñci* (the union of the lovers), *pālai* (separation), *marutam* (feigned love quarrels), *mullai* (patient waiting of the heroine), and *neytal* (the lamentation in separation).
