# Blossom 14 — My Son? · continued

> Where the succeeding word  
> Is the human generic class [kinship denoting] noun,  
> The sixth case declension *atu* of the preceding word  
> Gets dropped,  
> And the fourth case morpheme *ku* takes its place.
>
> (*Collatikāram: Vēṟṟumai Mayaṅkiyal*, verse 11)
