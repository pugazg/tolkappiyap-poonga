# Blossom 33 — He Who has Turned a Hero-stone

The wars waged by the ancient Tamils to either capture the cows or to retrieve the cows were fought with codes of conduct. From which direction is he throwing the spear or for that matter, from which direction is he shooting the arrows? In the event of the warrors confronting each other in the warfield, what was the method employed to distinguish between our men and the enemy?

> Wearing of palmyra, morgosa and mountain ebony,  
> The [totemic] flowers of towering note,  
> That mark identity and distinctiveness  
> Among soldiers who make the massive armies  
> Which the great monarchs of burning hostility commanded.

To distinguish between the enemy and one’s own soldiers, during face-to-face confrontation, the soldiers were made to wear the garlands of male palmyra tree, neem tree and common mountain ebony tree.
