# Blossom 15 — The Dream of a Eunuch

In the prebifurcated North Arcot District, all the bisexuals from various parts of the country converged for the annual *Kūvākam* festival commemorating the sacrifice of Aravāṉ. In the festival marked by gaiety, eunuchs, dressed in colourful makeups and dresses, performed the traditional *kummi* dance in various groups, much to the amusement of gasping youngsters and oldies alike.

The mythology goes that Aravāṉ was sacrificed at the commencement of the *Mahābāratha* war, and the bisexuals mourned his death with the beating of breasts and the rituals of removing their sacred thread and the *sindhur* mark on the forehead.

The festival characterized by joyous revelry came to an end as if the end of a storm and the bisexuals all exhausted retired to their restrooms to sleep. A bisexual named Rātā, who was in deep sleep marked by high decibel snoring, had a dream. The eunuch bore the name ‘Rātā’ - common to both male and female persons.

The scene in the dream figured Lord Kaṇṇaṉ and the five Pāṇṭavas with their consort Pāñcāli. As per the condition, the Pāṇṭavas had to live incognito in which they had to be in disguise so that nobody could identify them. Otherwise, they would not regain the countries lost to Turiyōtaṉa by Tarumaṉ in gambling. Therefore, each of the Pāṇṭavas was studiedly sporting the guise by which to hide from public gaze. Among them, Arjuṉaṉ, who was under the cloak of a bisexual, met Lord Kaṇṇaṉ, separately. Yes, all this happened in eunuch Rātā’s dream!
