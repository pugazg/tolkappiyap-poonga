# Blossom 70 — The Eight Emotions

Five and twenty years ago,

When Pūmpukār had been recreated  
With beauty and splendour blending,  
With art and artist at their best,  
At a function marked by grandeur,  
In the assembly of poets celebrating Tamil  
The lines I recited from my poem I recall  
And make them a beautiful archway to  
*Tolkāppiyam Meyppāṭṭiyal*.

The eight kinds of manifest emotions treated therein are:  
Laughter, grief, disgust, wonder  
Fear, pride, anger and joy.

By treading the path shown by  
The father of grammar in Tamil,  
Let us happily relish the eight sentiments:  
Laughter, grief, disgust, wonder,  
Fear, pride, anger and joy.
