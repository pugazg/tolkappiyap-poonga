# Blossom 50 — The Light of Home

The home is all music;

The hearts of the inmates all are things lustrous;  
Their minds are all books of knowledge;  
The letters of the books are marks of joy;  
*The woods are a spectacle of dance.*

If it is so for crows, sparrows and cows  
Need we say anything about men?

The revolutionary poet Pāratitācaṉ’s picture of a home it is!  
And our ripe author of antiquity Tolkāppiyar  
Has drawn a similar picture two thousand years ago.

The *Poruḷatikāram, Karpiyal* section of *Tolkāppiyam*  
Shows the two lamps at home  
That like blossoms of a creeper diffuse fragrance around.

The wedded life sweet suffers no want as it were;  
*And yet the man tells his parrot-tongued wife,*  
“Don’t you feel the want of a child at home?”
