# Blossom 67 — The Truth Unknown to the Devotee · continued

> Designating sights of flora and fauna in nature  
> That are evocative of love and tenderness  
> Is intended to bring comfort and reassurance  
> To the heroine in lonesome anguish.

These are different layers of meaning of the term ‘*iṟaicci*’ listed in the verses 35, 36, 37 in *Poruḷiyal*. The father and son were discussing them.

1. ‘*Iṟaicci*’ refers to the suggested meaning.
2. The parallel meaning suggested is context-specific and is obvious to the learned.
3. *Iṟaicci* functions to privilege love in times of distress and it underlines forbearance.

> *Iṟaicci* is  
> The suggestive import  
> Paralleling the Akam thematic strand explicit.
>
> That from *iṟaicci*  
> There emerges an implied subtext  
> Beside its evocative designation of flora and fauna  
> Will be obvious to men of discernment.
>
> Designating sights of flora and fauna in nature  
> That are evocative of love and tenderness  
> Is intended to bring comfort and reassurance  
> To the heroine in lonesome anguish.
