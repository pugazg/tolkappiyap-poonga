# Blossom 100 — The Hundredth Blossom · continued

> Pregnant with two interpretive possibilities;  
> Pondering a meaning  
> More appropriate than the one on hand;  
> Such are the devices  
> And others apropos thereto too  
> As must go inhering in the text of composition,  
> Brevity and perspicuity marking them all --  
> So do the literate men speak  
> Of the defining features of a composition.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Marapiyal*, verse 111)

Tolkāppiyam predates both Pāṇini and Kaudilyar. Having enjoyed myself in the literary and linguistic riches of this great work, I did invite you to go into this richly endowed garden of flowers.

Because it is against the Tamil culture to send back the visiting guests empty-handed, I have generously placed on your nectarous hands a hundred blossoms.
