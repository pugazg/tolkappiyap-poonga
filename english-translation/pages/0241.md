# Blossom 84 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Fifth Stage of Physical Manifestations · continued

Having already publicized your bond of passion,  
You have come now to us  
Saying the marriage awaits to be performed.  
Our kinsmen, the townsfolk and all know of it already!  
Is it proper that  
You bring it to us, your parents, so late?”  
The heroine’s confidante speaks  
In a tone soliciting,  
“O mother!  
Things have gone beyond reckoning;  
Pray, don’t put any obstacles now!  
She will keep the honour of the family unimpaired!  
And will bring no disgrace to your standing!”  
Speechless, the mother glances at the father.  
“Like mother like daughter;  
Do nod your head without any hesitation,” the father retorts.  
“My head has been nodding all these days,” so saying she smiles.  
Isn’t she the old lady who years ago had that bewitching smile?  
With the mother giving consent, the father follows suit.  
“Now, what prevents us from caressing each other, come”  
Was the invite of the hero.  
“Take to your heels, it won’t happen now,”  
The heroine says like a gentle breeze.  
“How come the shyness and reserve that have been in hiding have surfaced today?”  
He was surprised.  
“To refuse to frolic around is an experience sweet to me.  
Whence the shyness and tenderness?”  
Though refusing fleshly union,  
She relished the beauty of her lover to her heart’s content from where she was hiding.  
Not to be outdone he tiptoed to where she was to hug her.
