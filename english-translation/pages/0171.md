# Blossom 50 — The Light of Home · continued

> Being given the ceremonial oil-bath  
> After she has borne him a son;
>
> As he comes after acts of debauchery  
> And entreats the bliss of heroine’s love  
> Holding her tender feet in his hands,  
> While she in a mood of variance  
> Stays lying despondent on the bed  
> With the soft pillow for her prop;
>
> As he brings in the intercession of other women  
> And resolves the sulking of the heroine  
> When she remains hard of access  
> In a mode of intense variance;
>
> ....
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Karpiyal*, verse 5)
