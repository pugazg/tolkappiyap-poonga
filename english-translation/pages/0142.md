# Blossom 42 — Speaking through the Eyes · continued

Fortifying male resolve and female reserve!  
May each day of your rendezvous  
Be as blessed as this day’s!”  
The confidant greeted the hero thus  
In keeping with the Tamil culture.

***

> The meeting of eyes is the pointer sure  
> Suggesting the union of minds.
>
> Where the glances of the eyes reciprocate,  
> There marks the sprouting of a love career,  
> So has it been said.
>
> Nobility of bearing and fortitude  
> Are such stuff as marks the male [hero].
>
> Timidity, bashfulness and credulity  
> Are the ruling traits in a woman [heroine].
>
> (*Poruḷatikāram : Kaḷaviyal*, verses 5, 6, 7, 8)
