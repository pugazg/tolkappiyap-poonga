# Blossom 51 — Kumaraṉ’s Message

The function marking the birth of a child

Kumaraṉ and Kumari’s house  
Was visited by Vēḷaṉ from Kōṭṭai street.  
He joyfully gifted the chain made of cow’s horn.

“What Vēḷaṉ? Quite uncharacteristic of you to have come all alone?  
Why didn’t you bring your woman?” They eagerly enquired.

“We have not been on talking terms for some days.  
You have often been grumbling about my being issueless.’  
So saying,  
She had gone to grandma’s house.  
Six weeks have passed since then.”
