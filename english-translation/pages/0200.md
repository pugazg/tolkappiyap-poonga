# Blossom 65 — Would the Answer Emerge?

“Keeping you by me,  
The fiery sun felt cool;

Parted from you  
The cool moon did scorch me.

We bared out our hearts  
And communicated with each other.

Without pouncing upon me, much like a tiger,  
Do give a thought to my parents’ concern for my state.

Our clandestine love career has been exposed.  
The entire town is resounding with words of scandal about us.

I conveyed to you my willingness to elope with you.  
But you insisted upon marriage to the knowledge of one and all.
