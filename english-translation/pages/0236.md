# Blossom 82 — Fivefold Akam Love: The Third Stage of Physical Manifestations

As her dress gets loosened,  
She goes behind the fragrant creeper by the stream  
To adjust the clothes.  
Be it so that he would be her husband in future,  
Shyness prevents her from doing it under his gaze.  
The lustrous lass stands behind the creeper  
Loosens the attire all and rerobes it.  
She plaits the wear under the navel
