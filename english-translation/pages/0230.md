# Blossom 79 — Four Sources of Happiness

Though not as mammoth as a mountain, the required yield from the paddy fields to one’s heart’s content, the ripeness of mind acquired through learning, the communion of the hearts of men, the villagers assembling to dance and sing together in rapture-this is happiness earned to one’s own heart’s fill rather than the sadistic pleasure derived from others’ misery. This happiness manifests itself in all its serenity on the day of *Taip Poṅkal* which the Tamiḻs celebrate as a day of happiness with lots of emotions. The following musical verse captures the sentiment above:

Hail *Poṅkal*, hail *Poṅkal!*  
*Poṅkal* which exudes mirth  
everywhere.

Hail *Poṅkal*, hail *Poṅkal*  
Toiling gods of folks!  
Tens of thousands of people you prop up.

The world has placed the crown upon your head -  
Your roar of resolution can dwarf even the peaks of the Himalayas.

Let us all speak of the greatness of the peasants,  
And wish them for their prosperity!
