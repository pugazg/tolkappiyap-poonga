# Blossom 74 — Four Sources of Disgust

## Decrepitude/Senility (mūppu)

She bore *him for ten months, and suckled him.* After sending him to school, she took pride upon his growth. She became ecstatic on seeing her son in groom’s apparel. Now she is gifted with grandchildren too. Sapped of vitality with hair all grey, toothless and hunchbacked, she dreamt of spending her last days under the protective and loving care of her son. Her own son now denounces her, “Why are you still alive? Do you wish to make life miserable for me? Get lost and go to an old age home.” But even at this point what the mother regrets is her senility and not the behaviour of her son.

## Disease (piṇi)

In his youthful days, he slogged it out and earned money in abundance; all of a sudden, he was struck with epilepsy. Each day was marked by seeking medicine and treatment. The woman who joined him as his lifemate savoured every moment of their sunny days. Once he became an invalid, she became sick of marital life. Whenever he called out to her affectionately, she retorted derisively, “What the hell do you want? Speak out.” She kept scorching him. But he never reprimanded her. Instead, he was furious and would vent all his anger at the disease.
