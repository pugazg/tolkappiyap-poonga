# Blossom 41 — That Angel - Who Is She?

The confidant, though mischievous, is virtuous by disposition. His affection for the hero is boundless much like the waves of the sea getting gravitated towards the shore.

At dusk, the friend saw the hero surging ahead. He called him by clapping,  
“Why such an undue haste as if you were heading for a feast?  
I am back after seeing an angel under the waning moonlight.  
She is none but a goddess; I witnessed the scene of her stepping out of the temple and standing near the entrance.”

After hearing this from his friend,  
The hero resolved to see her. He literally flew towards the direction pointed at by the friend, like a kite pouncing upon a parrot.

Without even an atom of doubt, she is a goddess!  
Decked in the ornaments,  
She appeared before him.  
Finding her as an embodiment of beauty,  
He surged quite close towards her, in measured steps.

Since a goddess does not speak,  
There was no opportunity for him to speak out and clear his doubt.  
He dared to analyze her through eyes and see whether she is a goddess.
