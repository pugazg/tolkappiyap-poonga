# Blossom 27 — Joy in Adversity · continued

> Your daughter is to you  
> What the enchanting melody,  
> The seven strings in unison issue,  
> Is to the *yāḻ* where it’s conceived;  
> It’s beneficial to the songsters only.

The lover and his love after surmounting the difficult problems settle down in life. Realizing that they were leading a virtuous life, the foster-mother abandoned her search. It is natural that she would visit them, stay with them and attain heartfelt pleasure. Such would be the collective essence of *Tolkāppiyam Poruḷatikāram* and *Kalittokai*.
