# Blossom 13 — Commercial Competency

He was clad in a pure hand-woven *dhoti*, with a customary towel on the shoulder. He was immune to sweat and shiver. Beside him were his middle-aged wife in Ciṉṉāḷapaṭṭi sari, and their darling daughter Kaṇṇaki with a few books in her hands, a girl pursuing her education in a college. This compact family entered the busy Gokhale Bookshop after visiting some other bookshops on the street. Incidentally, the name of the owner of the shop was also Gokhale.

He gave an appearance of a staunch nationalist.

His son Aruṇaṉ with a silk shirt and a costly watch was frequently combing his curly hair with his hands.

He was also assisting his father actively in his business. But then, ever since Kaṇṇaki entered the shop, he paid hardly any attention to the business.

His father Gokhale seized the initiative from him and enquired Kaṇṇaki’s father enthusiastically, “Which book do you want?”
