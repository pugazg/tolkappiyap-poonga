# Blossom 96 — The Five Elements

> A structured blend of  
> Earth, fire, water, air and space  
> As the world is,  
> The use words are made of  
> Must be undistorted,  
> Undeviating from the norms  
> That go with the classifications of  
> The human -- non-human generic distinction,  
> And the five genders thereof.

(*Poruḷatikāram : Marapiyal*, verse 91)

By way of interpreting this verse, Iḷampūraṇar observes that the world is composed of the five elements, viz., earth, fire, water, air and space. He then goes on to explain the two words in the verse *kalattal* and *mayakkam*. The former is a mix, as of pearl, coral, sapphire and ruby. The latter signifies the one like the melted blend of gold, silver and copper.

The world, therefore, exists at two levels - one as a mixture, and the other as an undifferentiated one made up of the five elements, which the compound term in the verse *kalanta mayakkam* signifies. Thus goes the explanation of the commentator.
