# Blossom 2 — Vowels and Consonants

The teacher asked the students in the classroom, “How would you remember the vowels in Tamil?”

“Sir, shall I answer?” said student Maṇivācakaṉ.

The teacher nodded ‘yes’.

“I would remember the twelve-handed Murukaṉ to give me the cue”, pat came the reply.

“Oh, good! You evoke spirituality”, smiled the teacher.

“Sir, kuṟiñci flowers blossom once in twelve years. That would remind me of the twelve vowel letters”, was the reply of Pūṅkōti, a girl student.

The teacher took turns to question a farmer’s son, “O boy! What would you do to remember the vowels?”

“Sir, the month of Thai ushers in the Poṅkal festival which is marked by new clothes, cleaning houses, worshipping paddy and decorating bullocks. After celebrating the festival this year we would have to wait for twelve long months for its recurrence. In the same manner, the twelve vowels would be etched on my mind.”

The teacher posed his next question, “Okay, how would you remember the consonants?”
