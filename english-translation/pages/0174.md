# Blossom 51 — Kumaraṉ’s Message · continued

And a finer thing it would be were it to be freed of blame.”  
I also said.

“Vēḷaṉ! This is for you also:  
‘Even if there is no child in the house,  
We should toil like the child of the nation’.  
You should make a vow,  
And with a bright and beaming face,  
Lead a life like a sword and its sheath.”

Vēḷaṉ and his wife expressed their gratitude to Kumaraṉ  
And said, “We shall live together ever.”
