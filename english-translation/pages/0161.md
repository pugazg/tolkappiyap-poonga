# Blossom 48 — The Aiyar Conceived the Marriage · continued

The boy responded,  
“Well said, sire!  
We would remain unseparated  
Even in the world after death.  
Do take it from me!”

The headman said,  
“The times are changing  
And deceit looks raising its ugly head.  
Let’s summon the *Aiyar*.”

The *Aiyar* appeared there forthwith  
Asking about the reason for the summon.

One in the assembly wondered:  
“Should we need the *Aiyar* here?  
He belongs to an alien caste.”

The village headman explained then:  
“Dear friend!  
He is not the *Aiyar* you suppose;  
Be it clear in your mind,  
He is the one venerable amongst us,  
The one fit to sit at the head  
Capable of prescribing things righteous  
And prohibiting ways evil.

He has conceived of marriage  
That would guard against  
Falsity and deceit in love relations.”

The *Aiyar*, unblemished, stood up and spoke then,  
“Bring here the relations of the lovers  
Including their parents.”

Prompt they came.  
“Do you consent to the union of these lovers?”  
*Aiyar* asked.  
They nodded their heads in approval.
